# 12. Starting and running a business: don't lose the family savings

This chapter counts money and legal liability. Opening a shop, forming a company - at which step can your own money, the family's money and your spouse's money all go down with it? What does the law say? Can it be walled off in advance? This chapter answers those questions without converting anything into lifespan. Ordering: what most easily loses the family savings, yet is cheapest to avoid, comes first. So the signing entries come first and the do-it-yourself methods later. Entries written from experience alone, without literature, are marked C.

### 1. Start a business only with money you can afford to lose - don't touch the family savings, don't borrow to open
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Before acting, set yourself a number: if this money all went to zero, your mortgage, kids and medical care would be unaffected. Invest nothing beyond that number. Family members' money and borrowed money don't count toward it. The hard part is not adding more when the business looks promising.
- TL;DR: A sole proprietor's debts are repaid from money and things in your own name. If it can't be told whether the whole family ran it together, the whole family's property pays. A company isn't safe either: when the company can't pay its debts, the capital contribution you promised but whose deadline hasn't arrived can be demanded early. So set the investment by "losing it all still leaves the mortgage and medical care fine".
- Benefit: Losing isn't just money gone. A sole proprietorship's debts, if you ran the business alone, are repaid from money and things in your own name (borne with personal property). If the whole family ran it together, or it can't be told who ran it, the whole family's money and things pay (borne with family property). A debt both spouses signed is a joint debt repaid by both - and so is one a spouse ratified afterward. Money one side borrowed in their own name, where the creditor proves it went into a business the couple ran together, is likewise repaid by both. The company side has one more rule: when the company can't pay matured debts, a creditor can demand early payment of the money a shareholder promised in registered capital whose payment deadline hasn't arrived (subscribed contribution) (nationwide)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Articles 56, 1064). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> ; NPC Standing Committee (2023). Company Law (2023 revision, Articles 47, 54). <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: "Affordable to lose" means: with this money all gone, you still don't sell the home or borrow from relatives to plug the hole. Official statistics on how many businesses survive and how long on average - this time no verifiable original text was found on the National Bureau of Statistics or market-regulation sites. So this entry writes no numbers, only legal consequences, and is marked B.

### 2. Don't sign a personal guarantee for company loans - and your spouse should sign even less
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. When a bank, supplier or landlord asks you to sign a "shareholder personal joint-and-several guarantee" or a "spouse signature", pause first. Signing that page means personally giving up the "company debts don't chase the person" protection (waiving limited liability). Don't sign if you can avoid it. If you must, sign only a "general guarantee" and write down the maximum you're guaranteeing. The hard part: the other side often says "no signature, no disbursement" - hold up under it.
- TL;DR: Sign a "joint-and-several liability guarantee" and, when the company can't pay, the creditor can skip the company and come straight to you. "General guarantee" is what makes them sue the company first - you pay only what remains after its property is sold. A spouse signing along puts both spouses' family property in, and the "limited" of a limited company stops applying to you personally.
- Benefit: That sentence on the guarantee means "if the borrower doesn't pay, you pay". Written as "joint-and-several liability guarantee", the creditor may skip the company and come straight to your door. Written as "general guarantee", the creditor must first sue the company or arbitrate, then have a court force-sell the company's property - you make up only the shortfall. A debt both spouses signed is a joint debt repaid by both, and so is one ratified afterward. A spouse's signature puts both spouses' family property in (nationwide, from 2021)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Articles 681, 687, 688, 1064). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: Banks lending to small and micro businesses almost always demand a shareholder's personal joint-and-several guarantee. So this entry isn't telling you not to borrow - it's telling you to understand what that page signs away before signing. Then press the guaranteed amount under the "affordable to lose" number from entry 1 (start only with money you can afford to lose). The general rules for IOUs and guarantees: entry 18 of chapter 8.

### 3. Choose the right entity before opening: sole proprietors and general partners pay to the bottom - only a limited company is "limited"
<!-- tags: money=low time=mid will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Registration is done on the local government-services website, a few hours once. After opening a limited company you must keep books and file taxes every month - a lasting expense: time if you do it yourself, money if you hire a bookkeeper; prices differ by place.
- TL;DR: Sole proprietors and general partners pay to the bottom - however much is owed is repaid. A limited company's shareholders lose only what they promised to contribute at registration. But whatever was promised must genuinely be paid within 5 years: write 1 million and you may owe 1 million. Mixing the company account with the household account also kills "limited".
- Benefit: A sole proprietorship's debts are repaid from personal money and things, or the whole family's. Partners in a general partnership bear unlimited joint-and-several liability for the partnership's debts - however much is owed is repaid, and it isn't over until it's repaid; the creditor may also seize on just one partner and demand the whole amount from them. A limited partnership differs: its limited partners lose only up to what they promised to contribute (capped at subscribed contribution). A limited liability company's shareholders likewise lose only up to what they promised (capped at subscribed contribution). But "limited" fails in three situations. First, promised money must genuinely be paid in within 5 years of formation, and when the company can't pay matured debts, a creditor may demand it early. Second, money invested then pulled back out (evasion of contribution) must be returned. Third, using the company as a shell to dodge debts - including a single-shareholder company that can't prove the company's money is separate from the owner's. Under these, you personally repay the company's debts along with it (joint-and-several liability) (nationwide, from July 2024)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 56). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> ; NPC Standing Committee (2006). Partnership Enterprise Law (2006 revision, Article 2). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413955.htm> ; NPC Standing Committee (2023). Company Law (2023 revision, Articles 4, 23, 47, 53, 54). <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: "Limited" presupposes public and private kept distinct: company and personal accounts never mixed, no company money paying household expenses, complete books. Don't inflate registered capital for appearances. Whatever you subscribe must genuinely be paid within 5 years. Writing 1 million is 1 million of potential debt.

### 4. Don't be a nominal shareholder, and don't hold equity for someone else
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. When someone says "lend your ID to register as a shareholder" or "hold these shares in your name for now", refuse every time.
- TL;DR: With your name on the register, you are legally a shareholder. The money promised at registration must be paid in on schedule - failing that, you pay it plus the company's losses. If another shareholder didn't genuinely pay in at formation, you cover the shortfall too. The business is run by others; the debts land in your name.
- Benefit: A shareholder on the register is a legal shareholder. You must pay in every cent promised, on the company charter's schedule (paying subscribed contribution in full). Missing the deadline means paying it plus the company's losses. Where a shareholder didn't genuinely pay in at formation, the other founding shareholders repay within that gap (joint-and-several liability). When the company can't pay matured debts, a creditor may demand early payment of sums not yet due. The person really running the business isn't on the register - the debts are in your name (nationwide, from July 2024)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2023). Company Law (2023 revision, Articles 47, 49, 50, 54). <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: Whether a nominee agreement between the nominal shareholder and the real funder is valid, and who bears liability, is governed by the SPC's Provisions (III) on applying the Company Law. ### 5. Before franchising, check the commerce-ministry filing, get the written disclosure, and write the "cooling-off period" into the contract
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Go to the Ministry of Commerce's commercial-franchise information system website and check whether the brand is filed. Then require the other side, before signing, to give you the required disclosures in writing together with the contract text. Done in a few hours.
- TL;DR: By rule, the brand must first run 2 directly-operated stores for a full year, and file with the commerce authority within 15 days of the first franchise contract. Before signing, it must give you 12 items in writing - what the franchise fees are, how the deposit is refunded, whether existing stores make money. No filing found, materials refused, or money demanded before you may see the contract - walk away.
- Benefit: The rules require the brand (the "franchisor" in the regulation) to have at least 2 directly-owned and directly-operated stores, run for over a year. Within 15 days of the first franchise contract, it files with the commerce authority. Not filing: a 10,000-to-50,000-yuan fine; still unfiled after the deadline, 50,000 to 100,000 yuan plus public announcement. Before signing, it must hand you the required disclosures in writing - 12 items, including: which franchise fees, how much each; under what conditions the deposit is refunded; how many franchise stores exist now and how business is; abstracts of audited reports from the last 2 years; lawsuits and arbitrations from the last 5 years. The contract must also state that the franchisee may unilaterally rescind within a period after signing. If the brand conceals information or gives false information, the franchisee may rescind the contract (nationwide, from May 2007)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2007). Regulation on administering commercial franchising (State Council Order No. 485, Articles 7, 8, 12, 22, 23, 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_4179.htm> ; Ministry of Commerce (2012). Measures for administering commercial-franchise information disclosure (MOFCOM Order 2012 No. 2, Articles 5, 9). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2177025.htm> ; Ministry of Commerce. Commercial franchise information management system. <https://txjy.syggs.mofcom.gov.cn/>
- Notes: No filing found, disclosure refused, or money demanded before seeing the contract - walk away. The "cooling-off period" is those days after signing when you may still unilaterally walk. Its length is set by the contract - the regulation says only "a certain period" - so require the number of days to be written in when signing. For the brand's disclosed "average sales, gross and net profit of existing stores", press for where the numbers come from - the measures require the brand to state the source.

### 6. Before registering, settle the name, premises, business scope and registered capital - with materials complete, the license is issued on the spot
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: The registration step itself is free. Registration fees for enterprises and sole proprietorships were stopped on January 1, 2015. Do it yourself on the local government-services website or at the government hall - a few hours once. Seals, hired bookkeeping and renting a registered address (address hosting) are services bought on the market, not government fees.
- TL;DR: Registration itself is free - the fee was stopped in 2015. Only five items are submitted: the application, identity or qualification proof, premises documents, the charter or partnership agreement (not for sole proprietors), plus whatever else regulations name. Complete materials get the license on the spot; when on-the-spot isn't possible, within 3 working days. Money charged is mostly an agent's running fee.
- Benefit: The regulation fixes the materials at five items: "(1) the application; (2) the applicant's qualification documents and the natural person's identity proof; (3) documents for the domicile or main business premises; (4) the charter of the company, non-company enterprise legal person or farmers' specialized cooperative (union), or the partnership agreement; (5) other materials laws, administrative regulations and the State Council's market-regulation department require". In plain words: an application; something proving who you are or that you're qualified; something proving the shop or office is yours to use; the company charter or partnership agreement; plus whatever else regulations name. Companies also submit appointment documents and ID for the legal representative, directors, supervisors and senior management. Sole proprietors submit no charter. The name is "self-declared by the applicant under law" - you pick and report it yourself. Registered capital uses "subscription registration" - write down what you promise to contribute, no need to produce it on the spot. The business scope is picked from the registering authority's published categories. With materials complete and in proper form, registration is "confirmed and done on the spot". What can't finish on the spot finishes "within 3 working days", plus 3 more for complicated cases. "The business license's issue date is the market entity's formation date" - the date printed on the license is the company's or shop's birthday (nationwide, from March 2022)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2021). Regulation on market-entity registration administration (State Council Order No. 746, Articles 10, 13, 14, 16, 19, 21). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5636139.htm> ; SAMR (2022). Implementing rules for the market-entity registration regulation (SAMR Order No. 52, Articles 12, 25, 26). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/02/content_5676403.htm> ; Ministry of Finance and NDRC (2014). Notice on canceling, stopping and exempting a batch of administrative fees (Cai Shui [2014] No. 101, Annex 1). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2014-12/23/content_5023513.htm>
- Notes: Registration itself charges nothing - money charged is mostly an agent's running fee. Seal prices are set by the market. The State Council General Office explicitly "strictly prohibits designating seal makers", so "you must get the seal made at the designated place" is something you need not accept. How much registered capital to write doesn't affect appearances - it only sets your liability ceiling, and it must be paid within 5 years: entry 3 of this chapter (choose the right entity before opening). A business scope containing licensed items can't open before the license arrives: entry 7 of this chapter (licensed trades).

### 7. In licensed trades, don't open before the license arrives
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Before opening, check whether your business needs a license. Food service and food sales need a food-operation license. Selling only other people's factory-sealed packaged food needs no license - a filing instead. Open the doors only after the license is in hand.
- TL;DR: Opening without the license brings, at the light end, an order to stop, confiscation of what you earned, and a fine up to 10,000 yuan. If it's a monopoly or exclusive-sale good, or securities, futures, insurance or payment settlement, serious circumstances constitute illegal business operations - starting at up to 5 years. Renovating first and licensing later, or trial-operating before the license, is the most common misstep.
- Benefit: Doing business without the required license is investigated by the department the laws and regulations designate. Operating without a license where the law writes no specific penalty: ordered to stop, unlawful gains confiscated, plus a fine up to 10,000 yuan. There is a heavier tier: dealing in monopoly, exclusive-sale or trade-restricted goods without the license, or doing securities, futures, insurance or payment-settlement business without approval - serious circumstances constitute the crime of illegal business operations: up to 5 years' imprisonment or custody (short-term detention), plus a fine of 1 to 5 times the earnings; especially serious, over 5 years (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2017). Measures for investigating unlicensed operation (State Council Order No. 684, Articles 5, 6, 13). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-08/23/content_5219861.htm> ; SAMR (2023). Measures for food-operation licensing and filing (SAMR Order No. 78, Article 4). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html> ; National People's Congress (1997). Criminal Law (Article 225). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: Licensing conditions for finance, medical care and tutoring each have dedicated laws; this entry verified only the general investigation rules and criminal provisions. Milk-tea and snack shops count as food service and need the food-operation license. Renovating first and licensing later, or trial-operating before the license, is a common pit. Which tiers the food trade splits into and whether selling fresh meat and vegetables needs a license: entry 8 (in food, first see which tier you fall in).

### 8. In food, first see which tier you fall in: production and food service need a license, selling only prepackaged food switched to a filing, fresh meat and vegetables need nothing
<!-- tags: money=low time=mid will=no benefit=mid lens=freedom -->
- Cost: The license itself is free. But premises and facilities must meet standards - on-site inspection plus licensing takes a few weeks. Meat sellers must also keep each batch's quarantine certificate and meat-quality inspection certificate.
- TL;DR: Making things to sell - snack shops, braised food, baking, homemade pickles - all need the license. Selling only other people's factory-sealed packaged food needs no license, just a filing with the county-level-or-above market regulator. Fresh meat and vegetables need nothing. Operating unlicensed starts at a 50,000-to-100,000-yuan fine - higher than most small businesses' capital.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: the food business needs a license first. Producing food, selling food and running food service each require the license by law. "However, selling edible agricultural products and selling only prepackaged food do not require a license. Those selling only prepackaged food shall file with the food-safety supervision department of the local people's government at county level or above". Edible agricultural products are unprocessed things like fresh meat and vegetables; prepackaged food is made by others and sold sealed. "The specific measures for small food-production workshops and food vendors are made by each province, autonomous region and province-level city" - those two kinds follow provincial rules. Operating a food business without the license: everything earned, the food, and the tools, equipment and ingredients are all confiscated. Where the batch's total value at selling prices (goods value) is under 10,000 yuan, an additional fine of 50,000 to 100,000 yuan; at 10,000 or more, 10 to 20 times the goods value. Pig-slaughter regulation: pigs may be slaughtered only at state-designated plants, with official centralized quarantine. "Except for rural individuals slaughtering for their own consumption, no organization or individual may engage in pig slaughter without designation". "Pig products sold, used in meat-food production, or used by food-service operators and group-meal providers must come from designated slaughter plants and have passed quarantine and meat-quality inspection" - the pork you sell or use must come from designated plants and pass both checks. Privately slaughtering pigs outside a designated plant: ordered closed, with pigs, tools and earnings all confiscated, fined on the same scale as unlicensed food operation above. Animal-epidemic-prevention law: animal products sold and shipped must travel with quarantine certificates and marks; without them, correction is ordered plus a fine up to the goods value of equivalent qualified goods. Judicial interpretation: where food made or sold by small workshops and vendors fails food-safety standards and the buyer demands tenfold damages (punitive damages - the extra beyond a refund), the court shall support it (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2025, third amendment). Food Safety Law (Articles 35, 36, 122). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3> ; State Council (2021). Pig-slaughter regulation (State Council Order No. 742, Articles 2, 23, 31). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-07/22/content_5626534.htm> ; NPC Standing Committee (2021 revision). Animal Epidemic Prevention Law (Articles 51, 100). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080817703add2017737395a973e31> ; Supreme People's Court (2024). Interpretation on applying law in food-and-drug punitive-damages disputes (Fa Shi [2024] No. 9, Article 4). <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/bf531a70471f10d377788f28d3d8f8.html>
- Notes: Thresholds for small workshops, small food-service operations and vendors are set by each province - no specific numbers here. Go to your provincial market-regulation bureau's site and search "food small-workshop small-food-service small-vendor regulation" for the local version. Selling homemade food online doesn't drop a license because it's "online" - what needs licensing still needs it. Beyond licensing, this chapter's general rules apply: entity and license in entry 6 (settle name, premises and scope before registering), whether other trades need licenses in entry 7 (licensed trades). What the buyer's side can demand: entry 31 of chapter 5 (tenfold damages for food).

### 9. Bagging it makes it prepackaged food: production date, shelf life and ingredient list on the label, not one missing
<!-- tags: money=low time=mid will=no benefit=mid lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Printing labels costs cents each. Getting the date and ingredients right on every batch takes some time.
- TL;DR: Food sold in packaging is legally prepackaged food, the package must carry a label, and nine items are mandatory - production date and shelf life among them. Bulk food must also show name, date, and the seller's name and address. Missing mandatory items: under 10,000 yuan of goods value, a 5,000-to-50,000-yuan fine; at 10,000 or more, 5 to 10 times the value.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: prepackaged food - food sold in packaging - must carry a label on the package. The label states: name, specification, net content, production date; ingredient list; producer's name, address and contact; shelf life; product standard code; storage conditions; the generic national-standard names of food additives used; production-license number; and whatever else laws, regulations or food-safety standards require. Bulk food is sold unpackaged, weighed on demand. Selling bulk food also requires marking several things on the container and outer packaging: the food's name; production date or batch number; shelf life; and the producer/seller's name, address and contact. Making or selling prepackaged food without a label, or with a label or instructions violating the law: earnings and food confiscated together. Goods value under 10,000 yuan: an additional 5,000-to-50,000-yuan fine; at 10,000 or more, 5 to 10 times the value. Serious circumstances: ordered to halt production and business, up to license revocation. A label or instructions with only minor flaws that neither affect food safety nor mislead consumers: correction ordered first, and only refusal brings a fine up to 2,000 yuan. The new General Rules for Prepackaged Food Labeling is GB 7718-2025, published March 16, 2025 and effective March 16, 2027, with a two-year transition. It requires an independent area on the package showing production date and shelf-life expiry together, in high-contrast, easy-to-read type and color, ordered year-month-day (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2025, third amendment). Food Safety Law (Articles 67, 68, 125). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3> ; NHC and SAMR (2025). Announcement publishing GB 7718-2025 and 50 other food-safety national standards with 9 amendment sheets (2025 No. 2). <https://jkw.lasa.gov.cn/wswyh/wjzl/202504/a90766908ef24869a74f7373bf42463f.shtml> (full announcement reposted by Lhasa Health Commission) ; Supreme People's Court (2024). Interpretation on applying law in food-and-drug punitive-damages disputes (Fa Shi [2024] No. 9, Articles 6, 8). <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/bf531a70471f10d377788f28d3d8f8.html> (the tiering between label flaws and missing mandatory items) ; Beijing Municipal Market Regulation Bureau (2026). Can't find the production date? Food labels are changing - key points of the new rules. <https://scjgj.beijing.gov.cn/zwxx/scjgdt/202605/t20260507_4639154.html> (effective date, transition and new date-display requirements)
- Notes: Label problems come in two kinds, and fines and damages follow the split. One is minor flaws - typos, non-standard type size - that neither affect food safety nor mislead: the regulator only orders correction, refusal brings up to 2,000 yuan, and the buyer gets no tenfold damages. The other is mandatory items not marked - a missing production date or shelf life. The judicial interpretation says in writing this is not a minor flaw: the fine follows goods value - 5,000 to 50,000 yuan under 10,000, 5 to 10 times at 10,000 or more - and the buyer can separately demand ten times the food's price, with under 1,000 counted as 1,000 (see entry 31 of chapter 5, tenfold damages for food). For GB 7718-2025's item-by-item requirements, this entry verified only the dates and display method from the announcement and official explanation - follow the standard's original text for exact formats.

### 10. Ordinary food may not claim to cure disease: labels, instructions, ads and livestream scripts all count
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=mid lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Run the slogans, product-detail pages and livestream scripts through once. The hard part is resisting lines like "lowers the three highs" or "cures constipation".
- TL;DR: Ordinary food's labels, instructions and ads may not claim disease prevention or cure. Outside medical, drug and medical-device ads, no ad may claim treatment effects or use drug-sounding words. The fine is 1 to 3 times the ad spend; where ad spend can't be computed, 100,000 to 200,000 yuan. Serious cases can lose the business license.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: "labels and instructions of food and food additives may not contain false content and may not touch on disease prevention or treatment functions. Producers and operators are responsible for the content of the labels and instructions they provide". "Food ads must be truthful and lawful, may not contain false content, and may not touch on disease prevention or treatment functions. Food producers and operators are responsible for their food ads' truthfulness and lawfulness" - packaging, instructions and ads may not claim to prevent or cure disease, and the seller answers for whatever is written. Bragging about food in ads and deceiving consumers is fined under the Advertising Law, which also has a ban: "except medical, drug and medical-device ads, no other ad may touch on disease treatment functions, and none may use medical terminology or wording liable to confuse the promoted goods with drugs or medical devices". Violating it: the ad is ordered stopped; the advertiser must publicly clarify and undo the impact within the reach of the published ads; then a fine of 1 to 3 times the ad spend. Where ad spend can't be computed or was obviously understated, 100,000 to 200,000 yuan. Serious circumstances: 3 to 5 times the ad spend, or 200,000 to 1 million yuan - and the business license can be revoked, ending the business outright (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2025, third amendment). Food Safety Law (Articles 71, 73, 125, 140). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3> ; NPC Standing Committee (2021 amendment). Advertising Law (Articles 17, 58). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081817ab231eb017abd6bd860052d>
- Notes: "Health function" isn't something ordinary food may claim either. Health food may claim only functions from the state catalog, and the product must be registered or filed - ordinary food has no such path. How the buyer's side recognizes these scripts: entry 10 of chapter 6 (don't spend big money on supplements to condition your body). This entry governs more than packaging and ads: detail pages, livestream scripts and Moments posts count as ad content too - once posted, they leave traces.

### 11. The food trade has a criminal line: selling dead-or-diseased meat or over-limit goods is enough for a crime; mixing in toxic non-food material starts at five years regardless of amount
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Follow the routine in entry 8 (in food, first see which tier you fall in): keep quarantine certificates and purchase receipts, and put only things with checkable approval numbers in the ingredient list.
- TL;DR: The earlier entries fine money; this one takes people. Selling meat that died of disease, failed-inspection meat, or goods seriously over the limits for pesticide, veterinary-drug or heavy-metal residues: "sufficing" to cause serious poisoning is enough for the crime - nobody needs to actually fall ill - up to 3 years. Mixing toxic or harmful non-food materials into food starts at up to 5 years, no matter how much you sold.
- Benefit: Criminal Law Article 143 covers this tier. The food you make or sell fails food-safety standards. Where it "suffices to cause a serious food-poisoning accident or other serious food-borne disease", the crime stands: up to 3 years' imprisonment or custody (short-term detention), plus a fine. Seriously damaging health, or other serious circumstances: 3 to 7 years plus a fine. Especially serious consequences: 7 years to life, plus a fine or full confiscation of property. Article 144: mixing toxic or harmful non-food materials into food made or sold, or selling food knowing it was mixed in - up to 5 years plus a fine. Seriously damaging health, or other serious circumstances: 5 to 10 years plus a fine. Killing someone, or other especially serious circumstances: punished under Article 141 (10 years, life, or death). ### 15. Take money as "earnest money" (定金), never "deposit" (订金); write liquidated damages as a number; vet payment terms like a loan
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Write the word 定金 in the contract, at no more than 20% of the contract total. Take money before shipping. Before agreeing to let the other side take goods now and pay later (granting terms), vet them first.
- TL;DR: Only the word 定金 carries the double rule: if you break the deal, the money is gone; if the other side breaks it, they refund double. Anything beyond 20% of the contract amount doesn't count. Written as 订金, the law has no such rule. Granting terms means lending your money to the other side for free - if they collapse, it's a bad debt.
- Benefit: Earnest money counts only once actually handed over (formed upon actual delivery). The paying side breaking the deal forfeits it; the receiving side breaking it refunds the original plus an equal amount on top (double return). Anything beyond 20% of the main contract's subject amount isn't treated as earnest money. Agreed liquidated damages absurdly above or below the actual loss can be adjusted down or up by the court on a party's request. With both liquidated damages and earnest money in the contract, a real breach lets you pick one, not both (nationwide, from 2021)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Articles 585, 586, 587, 588). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: 订金 carries none of the earnest-money rules in the Civil Code - it's generally treated as a prepayment. That is the common saying in practice; this chapter hasn't verified case law. Payment terms mean lending your money to the other side interest-free; their collapse makes it a bad debt. ### 17. Pay wages per the contract and the law; raises, loans and bonuses all go in writing

### 20. Keep paperwork and supplier info for every batch, and don't buy stock priced obviously below market: when an employee buys the fakes, the boss is the one sentenced
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. For every batch, keep: the purchase contract, invoices, payment records, the supplier's business license and authorization documents. - TL;DR: Selling fakes - 30,000 yuan earned, or 50,000 sold - reaches the sentencing line: up to 3 years. Stock sitting unsold in the warehouse counts too. Keeping the purchase contract, invoices, payment records and supplier info is what keeps the no-damages road open.
- Benefit: Selling goods you know bear a counterfeited registered trademark reaches the sentencing threshold at 30,000 yuan of unlawful gains or 50,000 yuan sold: up to 3 years, plus or solely a fine. ### 22. Follow the procedures of your post; for good intentions beyond them, think first about whose responsibility it is
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=mid lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. At work, act by the rules, the doctor's orders, the process. Don't make decisions for others beyond your authority. Before helping, ask yourself: if something goes wrong, who answers. The hard part is withstanding the on-the-spot request to "bend the rules a little".
- TL;DR: Take nurses: the regulation writes that on finding a patient in critical condition, notify the physician at once; on finding an order violating the rules, raise it with the ordering physician. Other posts are the same - first see where your own procedures draw the "must report" line. The law doesn't ask anyone to leave someone to die either: voluntarily giving emergency aid that injures the person carries no damages.
- Benefit: Take nurses - the regulation writes the boundaries of the role like this. Finding a critical condition: notify the physician at once. In an emergency, to rescue a dying patient, necessary emergency aid may be given first. Finding an order that violates laws or diagnosis-and-treatment norms: raise it with the physician, reporting to the department head when necessary. The other side must also be written: the Civil Code provides that voluntarily giving emergency aid that ends up injuring the rescued person carries no civil liability for the rescuer. What the law protects is good-faith rescue - not "helping means paying" (nationwide)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: State Council (2008). Nurse Regulation (State Council Order No. 517, Article 17). <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6169.htm> ; National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 184). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: Within the post, follow procedures; emergency rescue is legally protected. For everything else, sort out whose authority and whose responsibility first - don't let private good intentions replace institutional decisions. ### 23. When it fails, exit through the proper procedure: simplified deregistration if eligible, bankruptcy if insolvent - never just leave it sitting
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Simplified deregistration you do yourself: post it on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System for 20 days. With debts still unpaid, the accounts must first be settled - collecting what's owed to you, paying what you owe; that step is liquidation. Bankruptcy takes a court application and months. The hard part: after losing, not wanting to face it, dragging on.
- TL;DR: With no debts and no wages, taxes or social insurance owed, all investors write a commitment, and after 20 days of publicity without objection, simplified deregistration goes through. Sole proprietors skip the publicity - 10 days without objection, direct deregistration. Can't pay matured debts: apply to a court for bankruptcy. Left sitting: the legal representative is barred for 3 years from being legal representative of another enterprise.
- Benefit: Nothing owed to or by others, or everything settled. No wages, social insurance or taxes owed. All investors have signed a written commitment. Meeting these, after 20 days of publicity without objection, simplified deregistration applies. Sole proprietors skip publicity - no objection from relevant departments within 10 days means direct deregistration. A company unable to pay matured debts, owing more than it holds (insolvent), may apply to a court for restructuring, settlement or bankruptcy liquidation. One already dissolved but unliquidated, owing more than it holds: whoever is responsible for liquidation shall apply for bankruptcy liquidation. The consequences of leaving it sitting: not publishing the annual report on time gets you listed in the abnormal-operation directory. Three years unpublished gets you into the seriously-violating enterprises list. The legal representative or person in charge may not serve as legal representative or person in charge of another enterprise for 3 years. And anyone already in the abnormal-operation directory can't even use simplified deregistration (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
