# 27. Pregnancy and childbirth: from finding out to discharge and paperwork

The first half of this chapter counts lives - the death risk of the mother and the fetus. The second half counts money and errand time. The two ledgers are not converted against each other. The content covers only "what to do when." Things without hard evidence or with tiny effects - prenatal nutrition, fetal education and the like - are not written here. The maternity allowance, maternity leave and the money ledger of raising a child are in chapter 18. Caring for the child after birth is in chapter 20. Emergencies are in chapter 13.

The World Health Organization's figures: in 2023, about 260,000 women worldwide died from pregnancy and childbirth. About 75% of them died from these five causes: severe bleeding after birth, infections, hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (a class of diseases where blood pressure rises during pregnancy, including preeclampsia and eclampsia), complications during delivery, and unsafe abortion. The WHO also says "most maternal deaths are preventable, as the medical solutions to prevent or manage complications are well known." The highest-impact entries in this chapter are the ones that head these problems off early.

### 1. From the moment you start trying, take 0.4 mg of folic acid every day until the first trimester is complete
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Tens of yuan a bottle. Rural women pay nothing - the national free folic acid supplementation program hands it out. One pill a day; the hard part is remembering it daily
- TL;DR: One folic acid pill a day around conception cuts the probability of a neural tube defect in the child by about seventy percent. Neural tube defects are birth defects like anencephaly and spina bifida. For someone who has already had one such child, the probability of the next one being affected drops to about a third of before.
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review pooled 5 trials and 6,708 deliveries and rated the evidence quality high. Folic acid supplementation around conception gave a fetal neural-tube-defect risk of RR 0.31 (95% CI 0.17 to 0.58, the credible range) - about a seventy percent reduction. For someone who had already had a child with a neural tube defect, the risk of recurrence in the next child was RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18 to 0.64) - down to about a third. Split by population group, 0.4 mg and higher doses showed no difference in effect. Folic acid alone versus a multivitamin showed no difference either
- Evidence: A
- Sources: De-Regil LM, Peña-Rosas JP, Fernández-Gaxiola AC, Rayco-Solon P (2015). Effects and safety of periconceptional oral folate supplementation for preventing birth defects. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (12), CD007950. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007950.pub3>
- Notes: The key is "start before conception." The fetal neural tube finishes closing within 28 days of conception. By the time a test tells you you're pregnant, most of that window has passed. So if you're planning a child, start 3 months ahead. In the same pooled study, folic acid showed no clear effect on cleft lip and palate, congenital heart disease or miscarriage - don't expect it to fix anything else

### 2. Before week 13, register a Mother and Child Health Handbook at the community health service center, and use up the free prenatal-check quota
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no benefit=mid lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Free - this is part of the national basic public health service program. It takes a few trips by gestational week
- TL;DR: At least five prenatal checks during pregnancy, plus a postnatal home visit and the 42-day check - the state pays for all of it. The way to get it: register a Mother and Child Health Handbook at the community before week 13 of pregnancy. Skip the registration, and these free visits are wasted.
- Benefit: The "National basic public health service specification (third edition)" includes maternal health management. Resident pregnant women in the jurisdiction can receive: 1 health check in early pregnancy, 2 in mid-pregnancy, 2 in late pregnancy, 1 postnatal visit, and 1 health check at 42 days after birth. Register the handbook before week 13, with the first prenatal check done at the same time. The two mid-pregnancy checks fall at weeks 16-20 and 21-24. The two late-pregnancy checks fall at weeks 28-36 and 37-40. The service lists published locally also include a full set of lab work at the first visit and a postpartum depression screen
- Evidence: A
- Sources: National Health and Family Planning Commission (2017). National basic public health service specification (third edition). <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf>; Jingjiang Municipal People's Government. National basic public health service program - maternal health management service (local service list disclosure). <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Notes: These 5 visits are the free floor, not the whole of prenatal care. The hospital will add ultrasounds, blood-sugar tests and fetal-heart monitoring as needed, and those are out of pocket. The earlier you register, the earlier the pregnancy risk grading is done. That grading sorts pregnant women by risk level; those found high-risk can be transferred early to a hospital that can handle them. If you live outside your registered district, register where you actually live - no need to go back to your household-registration place

### 3. At the first prenatal check, get the three tests - HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B; even a positive result has free blocking treatment
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Free - the mother-to-child transmission prevention program provides free testing
- TL;DR: At the first prenatal check, get HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B tested - the tests cost you nothing. A positive result isn't a dead end either. The state provides free medication and protocols to block the path of disease from mother to child - this is called mother-to-child transmission blocking.
- Benefit: Mother-to-child transmission means disease passing from mother to child. The "Work specification for preventing mother-to-child transmission of HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B (2020 edition)" requires: in early pregnancy or at the first prenatal check, the hospital informs you of the relevant information and "provides appropriate, standardized free testing." In other words, these three tests cost you nothing. Post-test counseling or referral follows the results. A positive result isn't a dead end either. Article 44 of the Regulation on AIDS prevention and control provides that pregnant women infected with HIV receive "free treatment and counseling to prevent mother-to-child transmission of AIDS." Article 43 requires blocking (cutting off the path to the child), treatment, postnatal visits, and infant follow-up and testing services. If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the newborn gets both the hepatitis B vaccine and hepatitis B immunoglobulin at birth (see entry 2 of chapter 20)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: General Office of the National Health Commission (2020). Work specification for preventing mother-to-child transmission of HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B (2020 edition). <https://wjw.xinjiang.gov.cn/hfpc/zhgl6/202012/08b7de496f0b40e586d4b99d00cf79c4.shtml> (reposted by the Health Commission of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region); State Council (2006). Regulation on AIDS prevention and control (Articles 43, 44). <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/zcwj2022/flfg/202304/t20230408_2992986.html> (reposted by the Beijing Municipal Health Commission)
- Notes: The earlier these three are tested, the more useful they are, because blocking and treatment both take time. Article 3 of the AIDS regulation also states plainly that "no organization or individual may discriminate against people infected with HIV, AIDS patients or their families" - their lawful rights to marry, work, receive medical care and attend school are protected by law. Everyday prevention and testing is in chapter 1. Post-exposure prophylaxis after a high-risk exposure is in chapter 13

### 4. For the whole pregnancy, not one cigarette and not one sip of alcohol - and the family shouldn't smoke indoors either
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=yes benefit=big lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Free - it even saves the cigarette and liquor money. The hard part is getting the people you live with to quit along with you
- TL;DR: Smoking during pregnancy pushes up the probabilities of preterm birth, low birth weight and cleft lip and palate. The risk of abnormal bleeding in pregnancy and delivery doubles outright. The risk of sudden infant death is higher too. Family smoking indoors counts the same. Quitting at any point still helps.
- Benefit: The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention's wording: smoking during pregnancy can leave the child "born small even at full term" and "possibly born early." It "doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery." It raises the risk of birth defects such as cleft lip and cleft palate. It also "damages the fetus's developing lungs and brain, with damage that can last into childhood." Babies of mothers who smoked during pregnancy, and babies exposed to smoke after birth, face a higher risk of sudden infant death syndrome. The official advice: "best to quit before pregnancy, but if you're already pregnant, quitting still helps" and "it's never too late to quit"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Smoking During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/pregnancy.html>
- Notes: Someone else smoking indoors exposes the pregnant woman to secondhand smoke, which counts the same for the fetus. So this entry is the whole family's business, not the pregnant woman's alone. Alcohol works the same way: no amount of drinking is known to be safe, and the easiest course during pregnancy is not one sip. What smoking and drinking cost adults themselves is in chapter 2

### 5. With high-risk factors for preeclampsia, one low-dose aspirin a day starting after week 12
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=some benefit=mid lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Tens of yuan. One tablet a day, kept up until delivery. The hard part is not forgetting
- TL;DR: Preeclampsia is a hypertensive disease of pregnancy. The high-risk people are these six kinds: preeclampsia in a previous pregnancy, a multiple pregnancy, chronic hypertension, diabetes before pregnancy, kidney disease, autoimmune disease. If you're one of them, one low-dose aspirin a day from week 12 lowers the probability of preeclampsia by about fifteen percent, preterm birth by about twenty percent, and the child dying around the time of birth by about twenty percent. If you're not in these groups, don't take it on your own.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force gives a grade B recommendation: for people at high risk of preeclampsia, start low-dose aspirin (81 mg/day) after week 12 of pregnancy as prevention. Preeclampsia is a hypertensive disease of pregnancy. Pooling multiple studies, the results are these - the 95% CI below is the credible range. Preeclampsia RR 0.85 (95% CI 0.75 to 0.95, 16 studies) - about 15% lower. Preterm birth RR 0.80 (95% CI 0.67 to 0.95, 13 studies) - about 20% lower. Perinatal death RR 0.79 (95% CI 0.66 to 0.96, 11 studies) - about 21% lower; perinatal death means death in the stretch around delivery. Small for gestational age / intrauterine growth restriction RR 0.82 (95% CI 0.68 to 0.99, 16 studies) - about 18% lower; that means the fetus growing smaller than others at the same gestational week
- Evidence: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Aspirin Use to Prevent Preeclampsia and Related Morbidity and Mortality: Preventive Medication. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/low-dose-aspirin-use-for-the-prevention-of-morbidity-and-mortality-from-preeclampsia-preventive-medication>
- Notes: What counts as high risk is these: preeclampsia in a previous pregnancy. Twins or a multiple pregnancy. Chronic hypertension. Type 1 or type 2 diabetes before pregnancy. Kidney disease. An autoimmune disease such as systemic lupus erythematosus or antiphospholipid syndrome. Anyone outside these groups should not take it on their own. This drug needs a doctor's prescription - lay out your history plainly at the prenatal check and let the doctor judge. What's mostly sold in China is the 100 mg enteric-coated tablet; take it as the doctor orders

### 6. At week 24 or later, do the gestational diabetes screen - don't grudge the sugar drink
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Tens to a hundred-some yuan. Go fasting, have blood drawn a few times, lose half a morning
- TL;DR: Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears only during pregnancy. After week 24, half a morning - one sugar drink and a few blood draws - finds it. Unchecked and unmanaged, it pushes up the risks of an oversized fetus, obstructed labor, the scalpel, preeclampsia and newborn low blood sugar.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force's grade B recommendation: screen asymptomatic pregnant women - those who feel nothing wrong - for gestational diabetes at week 24 or later. Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears only during pregnancy. If you screen only once, put it at week 24 or later. Whether screening before week 24 helps, the task force's conclusion is that the current evidence is insufficient to weigh benefit against harm (grade I). Unmanaged gestational diabetes pushes up the risks of these: macrosomia (an oversized fetus), obstructed labor, cesarean delivery, preeclampsia and neonatal hypoglycemia
- Evidence: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Gestational Diabetes: Screening. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/gestational-diabetes-screening>
- Notes: Of the people found with it, most control their blood sugar through diet and exercise; needing insulin is the minority. Don't skip this one visit for fear that a positive means injections. Blood sugar gets rechecked after delivery too: people who had gestational diabetes face a markedly higher risk of type 2 diabetes later

### 7. Memorize this "go to the hospital immediately" list - it holds during pregnancy and for a full year after birth
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. Five minutes to memorize; best if the people you live with know it too
- TL;DR: If any one of the following appears, go to the hospital that day - don't wait a night at home. A headache that won't lift or keeps worsening, changes in your vision, a fever of 38 °C. Severe swelling of hands or face, trouble breathing, chest pain or a racing heart. Severe abdominal pain that doesn't ease, fetal movement stopped or markedly reduced. Vaginal bleeding or leaking fluid. One arm or leg swollen, painful and red. Thoughts of harming yourself or the child. The full list is in the benefit field below. The same holds if any of them appears within a year after birth.
- Benefit: The urgent maternal warning signs listed by the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention are these. A headache that won't lift or keeps worsening. Dizziness or fainting. Changes in vision. A temperature of 38 °C or higher. Severe swelling of the hands or face. Thoughts of harming yourself or the child. Trouble breathing. Chest pain or a racing heart. Severe nausea and vomiting. Severe abdominal pain that doesn't ease. Fetal movement stopped or markedly reduced during pregnancy. Vaginal bleeding or leaking fluid during pregnancy. Abnormal vaginal bleeding or discharge after birth. One arm or leg severely swollen, red or painful. These problems can all occur within a year after birth - it's not "safe once the confinement month is over"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Hear Her - Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html>; World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality>
- Notes: The use of this list is separating "tough it out a bit more" from "must go right now." Headache plus blurred vision plus upper-abdominal pain points to preeclampsia - the hypertensive disease of pregnancy. One calf swollen and painful points to deep vein thrombosis (see chapter 13). Postpartum hemorrhage, in the WHO's words, can kill a healthy woman within hours. Any one item on the list means straight to the hospital - don't wait a night at home first

### 8. When your water breaks, lie flat where you are, raise your hips, and call 120 - no walking around, no shower
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Free
- TL;DR: Water breaking is not "pack your things slowly and then set out." The right move: lie down where you are, prop your hips up, and call 120. Standing and walking drains the amniotic fluid faster, and the umbilical cord can get flushed down with it - an emergency that can kill the baby.
- Benefit: The Beijing Municipal Health Commission's procedure: on discovering your water has broken, "immediately find a place to lie flat, to keep the amniotic fluid from flowing out" and "do not walk around at will," and "call the 120 emergency number at once." While waiting for the ambulance, tell the family to bring the things and meet you at the hospital. Medical institutions' outreach explains why: standing up drains the fluid faster, and the fetus's cord can slip out with it - called umbilical cord prolapse, an emergency that endangers the fetus. So lie down, prop your hips up, and the left-side position works
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Beijing Municipal Health Commission. Weekly first-aid topic: what to do when an expectant mother's water breaks early? <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jzjj/202001/t20200115_1575632.html>; Beijing Shunyi District Maternal and Child Health Hospital. What to do when your water breaks at home in late pregnancy? <https://www.bch-syfy.cn/Html/News/Articles/9453.html>
- Notes: The three signs that labor is near are regular contractions, the bloody show, and the water breaking. The first two let you pack at leisure; the water breaking is the only "lie down on the spot and call the car" one. Showering after the water breaks, squatting on the toilet, driving yourself - all classic wrong moves. Note the time the water broke and the color of the fluid, and tell the doctor. Yellow-green fluid, or fluid carrying meconium particles, suggests fetal distress - the fetus short of oxygen in the womb. With regular contractions, markedly reduced fetal movement, or fever and chills at the same time, stress to the doctor that this is an emergency

### 9. If you want painless labor, say so directly - it doesn't raise the risk of a cesarean
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=some benefit=mid lens=time -->
- Cost: A few hundred to over a thousand yuan, varying by place. Ask ahead whether the hospital and its anesthesia department offer it. The hard part is asking for it yourself
- TL;DR: Painless labor is an anesthetic shot in the lower back that presses down the pain of childbirth. It works better than other pain relief, and it doesn't make the scalpel more likely. "One shot of painless and your back aches for life" likewise has no basis. It now has its own billing item, and hospitals are more willing to offer it.
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review concluded: epidural analgesia is more effective than non-epidural methods at relieving labor pain and improving mothers' satisfaction with pain relief. Painless labor is epidural analgesia. The same review also says "epidural analgesia has no effect on cesarean-section risk or long-term back pain." As for instrumental delivery - the baby delivered with instruments - the increase it once showed hasn't appeared in studies since 2005. Since 2024, the national healthcare security administration's project-establishment guide for obstetric service pricing has given "labor analgesia," "doula-assisted delivery" and "family companionship during delivery" their own items. The reason is exactly that with no independent pricing item before, hospitals' willingness to provide labor analgesia was suppressed
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Anim-Somuah M, Smyth RMD, Cyna AM, Cuthbert A (2018). Epidural versus non-epidural or no analgesia for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD000331. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000331.pub4>; National Healthcare Security Administration (2024). Rebuilding the obstetric service pricing items to help build a birth-friendly society. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202406/content_6957553.htm>
- Notes: The two most widespread claims - "painless means back pain for life" and "painless means a hard labor and the scalpel" - both have no basis. Cochrane pooled epidural analgesia (the kind painless labor uses) against other methods and found it affects neither cesarean risk nor long-term back pain. The real side effects are these: lower blood pressure, fever, weak legs, inability to urinate, and a longer first and second stage of labor with oxytocin more likely needed. Whether to do it is your call - but don't give it up over rumors. Whether it's available depends on the hospital having an anesthesiologist on site around the clock - ask at the prenatal check

### 10. Without a medical indication, don't ask for a cesarean yourself - and don't schedule one to pick a lucky day
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=mid lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is withstanding what the family says
- TL;DR: The World Health Organization's position: once a place's cesarean rate passes 10%, deaths of mothers and newborns stop falling further. One that's needed must be done. One done to pick a date or dodge pain is just a major abdominal surgery taken for nothing.
- Benefit: The WHO's 2015 statement: when a population's cesarean rate rises to about 10%, maternal and newborn deaths fall. Beyond 10%, there is no evidence that mortality improves further. The WHO also stresses that every effort should be made to provide cesareans to women who need them, rather than striving for any particular rate
- Evidence: A
- Sources: World Health Organization (2015). WHO Statement on Caesarean Section Rates (WHO/RHR/15.02). <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-15.02>
- Notes: This entry is not against cesareans. When medicine genuinely requires one, it's a life-saving operation - do it, don't tough it out. What's opposed is the scalpel without a medical need: fear of pain, picking a lucky hour, calculating the child's school-entry age. The costs are booked here: a cesarean is major abdominal surgery, recovery is slower, and the next pregnancy carries higher risks of placenta previa, placenta accreta and uterine rupture. That directly bears on whether you still want a second child

### 11. Confirm your maternity-insurance enrollment before pregnancy - an unemployed spouse can also be reimbursed for childbirth medical costs
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. One call to the social-insurance or medical-insurance hotline
- TL;DR: If the employer pays maternity insurance, the medical costs of childbirth and the allowance during maternity leave both come out of that fund. A spouse without a job can also have the medical-cost part reimbursed. Whether you've contributed enough months, and whether giving birth in another city needs registration first - both have to be asked in advance; they can't be fixed afterward.
- Benefit: Article 54 of the Social Insurance Law: where the employer has paid maternity-insurance contributions, its employees enjoy maternity-insurance benefits. An employee's spouse without a job enjoys childbirth medical-expense benefits under state provisions, paid from the maternity-insurance fund. In other words, with the employer paying maternity insurance, the employee enjoys the benefits - and the medical costs when that employee's unemployed spouse gives birth can also come out of the fund. Maternity-insurance benefits come in two parts: one is childbirth medical expenses - the medical cost of the birth itself; the other is the maternity allowance - the money paid to you during maternity leave. Article 56 lists the three situations that qualify for the allowance: a female employee taking maternity leave for childbirth, taking leave for a family-planning procedure, and other situations provided by laws and regulations. The maternity allowance is "calculated and paid at the average monthly wage of employees of the employee's employer in the previous year" - it's counted on your employer's average wage last year, not your own wage
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2010). Social Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China (Articles 54, 56). <https://guangdong.chinatax.gov.cn/gdsw/qysw_gkwj/2020-02/12/content_8e8c485d0db34b989531f3ba57cec5a9.shtml> (reposted by the Guangdong Provincial Tax Service of the State Taxation Administration)
- Notes: Two things to ask in advance, because they can't be fixed afterward. One is whether you've contributed enough consecutive months: every place sets a minimum, and falling short means no benefits. The other is whether giving birth outside your insured place requires registering the out-of-town birth first: unregistered, you may have to pay the whole bill yourself and claim it manually afterward - or not be reimbursed at all. How maternity-leave days and the allowance are calculated is in entry 2 of chapter 18

### 12. Get the Medical Birth Certificate done before discharge - decide the name early and don't write a wrong character
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=time -->
- Cost: Free. Done in passing while handling the discharge paperwork
- TL;DR: This certificate is the prerequisite for household registration, insurance enrollment and school - and only the institution that delivered the baby can issue it the first time. Coming back to make it up after discharge means a separate process and extra materials. For the characters in the name, first confirm the household-registration system can type them.
- Benefit: Article 23 of the Maternal and Infant Health Care Law: medical and health institutions and personnel engaged in home delivery shall, in accordance with the provisions of the health administration department under the State Council, issue the uniformly printed medical birth certificate for newborns. This certificate is the precondition for everything after: household registration, insurance enrollment, the medical-insurance card, nursery and school admission all need it. And the first issuance can only be done by the delivering institution. Coming back after discharge means another process, with extra supporting materials
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (1994, amended 2017). Maternal and Infant Health Care Law of the People's Republic of China (Article 23). <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647619.htm>
- Notes: Before filling it in, check the parents' names, ID numbers and the child's name character by character. The characters in the name must be typeable in the household-registration system - obscure characters bring endless trouble. Changing the name or the certificate means reissuance or replacement, far costlier than checking on the spot. Once you have it, store it apart and safe - it's in the same class as the household register and the ID card

### 13. Don't refuse the newborn heel-prick screening or the hearing screening
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no benefit=big lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Free in most places, or tens of yuan. Some extended items are out of pocket. One heel prick after the birth
- TL;DR: One heel prick and one hearing test can catch hypothyroidism, phenylketonuria and hearing impairment while the child still looks completely normal. By the time symptoms show, the damage to intelligence can't be made back.
- Benefit: Article 3 of the Measures for the administration of newborn disease screening: "the diseases screened nationally for newborns include neonatal inherited metabolic diseases such as congenital hypothyroidism and phenylketonuria, and hearing impairment." These two kinds of disease share one trait: nothing looks wrong early on, and by the time symptoms are obvious, the damage to intelligence is irreversible. Early detection and intervention work very well - the means are thyroid hormone, special formula, hearing aids or cochlear implants. Article 10 requires that for a positive screening result, the institution promptly notify the guardian to confirm the diagnosis
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Ministry of Health (2009). Measures for the administration of newborn disease screening (Ministry of Health Order No. 64, Articles 3, 10, 11). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1371363.htm>
- Notes: Article 11 of the measures requires that before screening, the guardian be truthfully told the items, conditions, methods, sensitivity (how much it can catch) and cost, and sign consent. So you'll receive an informed-consent form. Before signing, read it: which diseases are the state-prescribed ones, which are self-paid extras. A positive first screen is not a diagnosis - the vast majority are ruled out by one recheck, so don't fall apart at this step. If the hearing first screen fails, go to the re-screen as notified; don't decide for yourself that "the child can hear, never mind"

### 14. The moment you have the birth certificate, enroll the child in resident medical insurance - don't wait for the household registration
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: One resident medical-insurance premium a year, a few hundred yuan
- TL;DR: With the birth certificate you can enroll the child online - no need to wait for the household registration. Once enrolled, the hospitalization bill at discharge is reimbursed on the spot. Newborn hospital bills are not small, and insured versus not makes a big difference.
- Benefit: The Chinese government website's 2024 wording: "a newborn can enroll online on the strength of the medical birth certificate" - no prior household registration needed. "After the newborn is discharged, medical expenses can be reimbursed directly." The old way was: register the household first, then enroll, with the hospitalization bill paid in full up front and claimed back afterward. The average time to complete enrollment and payment has also been cut from 28.7 working days at the start of the year to 6.4 working days
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Chinese government website (2024). State Council regular policy briefing on improving the long-term mechanism for basic medical-insurance enrollment. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202409/content_6973567.htm>
- Notes: Prematurity, jaundice phototherapy, neonatal pneumonia - these are the common reasons newborns are hospitalized, easily running to over ten thousand yuan, and insured versus not differs hugely. Localities' rules on "enroll within this many days of birth to enjoy benefits from the day of birth" are not quite the same. How it's executed, follow what the local medical-insurance department says. Calling 12393 before discharge is the easiest way to ask

### 15. Within one month of the birth, report the birth registration at the police station
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=small lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. One trip to the police station's registration window
- TL;DR: The law gives one month - not "whenever you get around to it." Household registration gates vaccinations, nursery, school and the ID card. The longer you drag, the more materials you have to make up.
- Benefit: Article 7 of the household-registration regulation: "within one month of an infant's birth, the head of household, a relative, a supporter or a neighbor shall report the birth for registration to the household-registration authority of the infant's habitual residence." This is a statutory time limit, not a suggestion. Household registration is the precondition for what follows: vaccinations, nursery, school, the ID card. The longer you drag, the more materials you have to make up
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (1958). Regulation on household registration of the People's Republic of China (Article 7). <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2531969.html> (reposted by the Guangdong Provincial People's Government)
- Notes: Bring everything: the medical birth certificate, both parents' ID cards and household registers, the marriage certificate. Registering with the father or the mother is your choice, but the two places' policies differ, and school district and insurance location are both affected - think it through before you go; changing it is a hassle. A child born outside marriage can likewise be registered

### 16. Don't skip the 42-day postpartum recheck - it doubles as the postpartum depression screen
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=mid lens=mortality -->
- Cost: Free - part of the basic public health service. It takes half a day. The hard part is not wanting to move right after the confinement month
- TL;DR: This recheck costs nothing and includes a complete blood count, a vaginal-discharge routine, and a postpartum depression screen. Serious problems can appear any time in the year after birth - don't assume you're safe once the confinement month is over.
- Benefit: The maternal health management in the "National basic public health service specification (third edition)" includes 1 postnatal visit and 1 health check at 42 days after birth. The service list published locally includes lab work (complete blood count, vaginal-discharge routine) and 1 postpartum depression screen at this visit. Serious complications can appear any time in the year after birth. In entry 7's "go to the hospital immediately" list, "thoughts of harming yourself or the child," "abnormal vaginal bleeding or discharge after birth" and "one limb severely swollen and painful" are all postpartum items

- Notes: Postpartum depression isn't being precious, and it isn't solved by a family member saying "cheer up." It has a proper screening questionnaire, it has treatment, and people recover. What to do when suicidal thoughts appear is in chapter 1. The lawful way out when you truly can't raise the child is in entry 20 of chapter 9. This recheck also looks at how the wound is healing, how the uterus is recovering, and how the pelvic floor functions.
