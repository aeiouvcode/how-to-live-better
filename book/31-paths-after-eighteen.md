# 31. The paths available after eighteen

This chapter counts money and time; the entry on refusing military service also bears on personal freedom. This is a map of paths, not a manual for any one of them. First the statutory thresholds of each path side by side. Then, entry by entry, the things only usable once known: what military service actually pays, which doors into the establishment exist besides the civil-service exam, which two majors at college-application time point directly at a staffed post, whether a degree can still be made up later, what kind of company to look for if you want to work abroad, how social insurance works for people who don't join an employer, and whether the state will lend you start-up money while paying part of the interest.

One thing up front. The paths available after eighteen go far beyond what this chapter covers. It includes only those with nationwide public documents whose thresholds and benefits can be checked item by item. State-owned enterprises, factories, seafaring, freelancing, continuing on to high school and university - these paths have no unified documents to check. That doesn't mean they can't be walked; it means there's no quotable hard provision, and this book won't write conditions for them from impressions.

Which path to take is your own business. This chapter supplies two things: which document the threshold is written in, and which deadlines and qualifications expire permanently once missed. How to count the study-versus-work question and how to pick a skill is in chapter 23. Overtime pay, work injury and severance once you're inside an employer are in chapter 19. The capital and the exit for opening a shop or a company are in chapter 12. Studying abroad is in chapter 32. The safety net after job loss is in chapter 7. None of that is repeated.

### 1. Check the thresholds first: the age and education requirements of twelve paths are all written in fixed text
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=time -->
- Cost: Free. Ten minutes to check yourself against each one.
- TL;DR: The age and education thresholds of these twelve paths are all written in fixed text. Working requires being 16. Military service is 18 for men, relaxed to 24 for bachelor's graduates. The civil-service exam wants ages 18 to 35 and a junior-college degree or above. The self-taught exam and registering as an individual business look at neither education nor age.
- Benefit: Military service, per the Military Service Law: "male citizens who have reached the age of eighteen shall be enlisted for active service; those not enlisted in that year may still be enlisted before the age of twenty-two. The enlistment age for graduates of regular institutions of higher education may be relaxed to twenty-four, and for graduate students to twenty-six." The civil-service exam, per Article 18 of the Provisions on civil servant recruitment: conditions include "age eighteen or above and under thirty-five" and "education at junior-college level or above." Both may be appropriately adjusted with approval by the civil-service authority at provincial level or above. Article 19 lists five kinds of people who may not apply - for example those criminally punished for an offense, those dismissed from public office, and those listed as jointly disciplined for serious dishonesty. Firefighters, per the Measures for recruiting firefighters for the national comprehensive fire and rescue force. Article 5: "age 18 or above and under 22" and "education at senior-secondary level or above." Article 6: "persons with junior-college education or above, and retired soldiers of the PLA and the People's Armed Police... the age may be relaxed to 24." Military civilian personnel, per Article 9 of the Regulation on civilian personnel of the Chinese People's Liberation Army: basic conditions include "having reached the age of 18." Article 19 states the maximum age for first-time recruitment into lower-grade posts is 35 - lower-grade posts are entry-level. Public institutions, per Article 8 of the Regulation on personnel management of public institutions: "public institutions shall recruit new staff openly from society." Individual businesses, per the Regulation on promoting the development of individual businesses: "citizens with business capacity... who register as individual businesses under law are governed by this regulation." The self-taught exam, per the Interim regulation on higher-education self-taught examinations. Article 3: "citizens of the People's Republic of China, regardless of sex, age, ethnicity, race or education already received, may take the higher-education self-taught examinations under this regulation." Article 20: "enrolled students of full-time schools of all kinds and levels may not apply." Publicly funded teacher-training students, per Article 2 of the Measures for publicly funded teacher education linking bachelor's and master's programs at the normal universities directly under the Ministry of Education. It recruits only at the six ministry-affiliated normal universities: Beijing Normal University, East China Normal University, Northeast Normal University, Central China Normal University, Shaanxi Normal University and Southwest University. The bachelor's and master's stages are designed as one, assessed in stages - bachelor's followed by the master's. Tuition and accommodation during study are borne by the central budget, plus a living-expense subsidy. Article 5: "recruitment of publicly funded teacher-training students uses the early-admission batch" - admitted ahead of the regular batch. Directed medical students, per the Notice on employment placement and contract-performance management for rural order-directed tuition-free medical students: "after admission and before receiving the enrollment notice, the student must sign an agreement with the training school and the county-level health administration and human-resources departments of the directed employment location." Working abroad, per the Regulation on the administration of foreign labor-service cooperation. Article 5: engaging in foreign labor-service cooperation requires approval by the commerce authority to obtain operating qualifications. Article 7, paragraph 4: "without lawfully obtaining a foreign labor-service cooperation qualification certificate and completing registration, no one may engage in foreign labor-service cooperation." In other words, a company without that certificate breaks the law by organizing people to work abroad. Article 8, paragraph 2: "no organization or individual may organize workers to work abroad in the name of business, tourism or study abroad." Platform gigs and flexible employment have no statutory education threshold; the basis for self-enrollment and occupational-injury protection is in entries 11 and 12 of this chapter. The Compulsory Education Law "prohibits employers from hiring school-age children and adolescents who should be receiving compulsory education" (nationwide)

### 2. In the year you turn 18, complete military-service registration by October 31; active service as a conscript is two years
<!-- tags: money=0 time=high will=yes benefit=mid lens=time -->
- Cost: Free. The registration is one online form, minutes to fill. Actually serving costs two full-time years in which nothing else can be done alongside.
- TL;DR: Military-service registration is a statutory duty, not enlistment - registering doesn't mean you must go. Men turning 18 in the current year must register online by October 31. If you don't, the county orders you to correct it within a time limit; persist, and it compels you to perform your military-service duty plus a fine. If you do serve, active service as a conscript is two years.
- Benefit: Registration, per the Provisions on military-service registration work: "the military-service organs of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities without districts, and municipal districts shall, by October 31 each year, organize initial military-service registration for male citizens in their administrative area who turn 18 by December 31 of that year." The same provisions: "initial military-service registration is mainly done online, completed by citizens themselves through the military-service registration platform." What happens if you don't register - Article 57, paragraph 1 of the Military Service Law, covering those who "refuse or evade military-service registration": "the county-level people's government orders correction within a time limit; failing correction by the deadline, the county-level people's government compels performance of the military-service duty and imposes a fine." The law also states: "the term of active service for conscripts is two years." "As the military needs and the person volunteers, citizens aged seventeen to eighteen may be enlisted for active service." A non-commissioned officer "who has served twelve full years of active service or meets other state conditions is assigned a job by the local people's government at or above county level of the placement place." One who "has served thirty full years of active service or reached the age of fifty-five or meets other state conditions is placed in retirement." Assigned a job means the government provides a job; retirement placement means retiring under retirement rules. The Regulation on conscription work states "priority is given to graduates of regular institutions of higher education and to conscripts with special political, physical or professional-skill requirements." It also states "applicant citizens who are full-time enrolled students or fresh graduates of regular institutions of higher education may apply at their pre-enrollment household-registration place or at their school's location" (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: General Office of the State Council and General Office of the Central Military Commission (2025). Provisions on military-service registration work (Guobanfa [2025] No. 41). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202512/content_7051363.htm>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2021). Military Service Law (2021 revision, Articles 20, 25, 26, 54, 57). <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm> (published by the Ministry of National Defense); State Council and Central Military Commission (2023). Regulation on conscription work (State Council and Central Military Commission Order No. 759, Article 5). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2023/content_5753313.htm>
- Notes: Registering and enlisting are two different things, a big price apart. The string of punishments including the two-year exit ban punishes only those who back out after enlisting - see entry 3 (refusing military service). Women are not required to do initial registration; they are enlisted as the military needs. Soldiers testing into military academies and well-performing soldiers promoted to officer rank have their conditions written in internal military regulations, not checkable outside. The Military Service Law writes only two sentences: active officers are selected from "active soldiers with outstanding performance" and the like, and military academies enrolling cadets are not bound by enlistment-age limits. This book gives no cut-off scores or quotas.

### 3. Refusing military service after enlisting: no exit or school enrollment for two years, and the civil service and state-owned enterprises are closed to you
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. The price is thinking through whether to go before signing up.
- TL;DR: Sign up and then refuse to serve, and the consequences come in a string. The civil service may not hire you. State-owned enterprises and public institutions may not recruit you. For two years: no exit from the country, no advancing to a higher school, no resuming your old school. You're also placed on the list of seriously dishonest subjects in national-defense duties. So "sign up first, drop out if it doesn't work" is a very expensive idea.
- Benefit: Article 57, paragraph 1, item 2 of the Military Service Law covers "applicant citizens who refuse or evade enlistment for active service." The punishment is the same: "the county-level people's government orders correction within a time limit; failing correction by the deadline, the county-level people's government compels performance of the military-service duty and imposes a fine." Paragraph 2 of the same article: "whoever commits the act in item 2 of the preceding paragraph and refuses to correct it may not be employed as a civil servant or as staff managed with reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China, may not be recruited or hired as staff of state-owned enterprises or public institutions, may not leave the country or advance to or resume schooling within two years, and is placed on the list of seriously dishonest subjects in national-defense duties for joint discipline." Joint discipline means multiple departments executing these restrictions together off the same list. Article 58 extends the same discipline to people already in the military. A serviceman who, aiming to evade service, refuses to perform duties or deserts faces consequences depending on the handling result: those struck from the rolls, expelled from military status or criminally prosecuted are punished per Article 57, paragraph 2; those struck from the rolls are additionally fined (nationwide, effective October 1, 2021)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2021). Military Service Law (2021 revision, Articles 57, 58). <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm> (published by the Ministry of National Defense)
- Notes: This entry counts personal freedom and employment eligibility, not money. Be precise about who is punished: this string of discipline punishes only two kinds of people. One, "applicant citizens who refuse or evade enlistment." Two, those struck from the rolls, expelled from military status or criminally prosecuted after joining. Someone who merely skipped military-service registration is not in it - registration is in entry 2. Punishment presupposes "refusing to correct," determined and imposed by the county-level people's government. The fine amounts are set locally; this book doesn't write them. This entry doesn't push you to go or not go - it only says that from the moment you sign up, this decision can't be taken back.

### 4. The two most concrete benefits for college students who serve: tuition reimbursed up to 20,000 yuan a year, enrollment kept for two years, and a dedicated quota for the graduate entrance exam
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. After discharge, submit the materials as your school requires - one or two trips.
- TL;DR: For enrolled students, fresh graduates and admitted freshmen who serve, the state pays the tuition for the service period. Up to 20,000 yuan per person per year for undergraduate and junior-college students, up to 25,000 for graduate students - at most forty or fifty thousand over two years. Your enrollment is kept, and within two years of discharge you may enroll or resume. The graduate entrance exam has a separate plan recruiting only retired college-student soldiers.
- Benefit: The 2024 notice from the Ministry of Finance and three other departments gives students who serve three benefits: tuition compensation, assumption of state student loans used for tuition, and tuition reduction. Assumption means the state repays the loan for you. The standards were "adjusted accordingly to no more than 20,000 yuan per person per year for undergraduate and junior-college students and no more than 25,000 yuan per person per year for graduate students," effective from the fall 2024 semester. How much exactly: "the higher of the tuition actually paid by the student or the state student loan obtained (principal plus all interest accrued before full repayment) applies." Plus one more sentence: "amounts beyond the standard are not compensated, assumed or reduced." The enrollment side, per the Law on protecting veterans: "a serviceman who, before enlistment, had been admitted by or was studying at a regular institution of higher education keeps the admission qualification or enrollment during active service, and may enroll or resume within two years of discharge, and may transfer to another major at the same institution under relevant state provisions." The law also states: "institutions of higher education may, under state overall arrangements, recruit veterans through separately listed plans and separate admissions." The graduate-exam route, per a 2026 notice from the Ministry of Education's general office. The plan is called the "retired college-student soldiers plan." The notice states: "separately allocated and dedicated within the national master's admissions total; it may not be converted into regular plans or other special plans. Each admitting unit sets its own... preliminary-exam score requirement (generally not above that for regular-plan candidates in the corresponding discipline)... under equal conditions, candidates with longer service are admitted first." "Separately allocated and dedicated" means these places are carved out to recruit only retired college-student soldiers. In 2026 the plan runs at 532 regular institutions of higher education (nationwide)

### 5. After discharge, only six situations get a government-assigned job; the self-employed must report within 30 days
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Bring your discharge certificate and make one trip to the veterans affairs department of the placement place.
- TL;DR: "Serve and get assigned a job" stopped being the general rule long ago. Now only six situations get a government-assigned job, and the vast majority of conscripts discharged after two years are not in those six. The tier without government assignment is called self-employment, and it has a hard deadline: report within 30 days of leaving active service. Without a proper reason, passing 30 days counts as waiving your placement benefits.
- Benefit: Article 25 of the Regulation on the placement of veterans lists six situations in which the placement-place people's government assigns a job. Verbatim: "(1) non-commissioned officers who have served 12 full years of active service; (2) those awarded a medal or honorary title during active service; (3) those awarded a third-class combat merit, second-class merit or higher during active service; (4) those receiving a first-class commendation during active service; (5) those disabled in war and assessed at disability grade 5 to 8; (6) children of martyrs." The regulation also states: "the placement-place people's government shall complete the job-assignment task for retired NCOs and conscripts within 6 months of receiving them." The employing unit "shall sign a medium- or long-term employment or labor contract of no less than 3 years with them as required. Among them, an enterprise receiving a retired NCO with over 10 years of service shall sign an open-ended labor contract." Open-ended means no end date on the contract. During the waiting period before assignment, "a living subsidy is paid monthly at the local monthly minimum-wage standard." If assignment doesn't happen on time for reasons not your own, "a living allowance of no less than 80% of the average wage of the unit's comparable staff is paid monthly until the post is taken up." Situations where job-assignment placement may not be used include expulsion from the Communist Party of China and criminal punishment. The self-employment deadline, per Article 51: "retired NCOs and conscripts placed in self-employment shall report to the veterans work authority of the placement-place people's government within 30 days of being approved to leave active service. Failing to report on time without a proper reason by more than 30 days is deemed a waiver of placement benefits." (nationwide, effective September 1, 2024)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council and Central Military Commission (2024). Regulation on the placement of veterans (State Council and Central Military Commission Order No. 787, promulgated July 29, 2024, effective September 1; Articles 25, 31, 32, 35, 51, 75). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11526/202408/content_6969192.html>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2020). Law on protecting veterans (Articles 13, 14, 15). <http://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/fgwx/flfg/16048597.html> (published by the Ministry of National Defense)
- Notes: On the day this regulation took effect, the 2011 Regulation on the placement of retired soldiers (Order No. 608) was repealed. Many online guides still cite the old one, whose conditions are phrased differently - the old text read "awarded second-class merit or above in peacetime or third-class merit or above in wartime." The 30-day deadline governs only the self-employment tier; other placement forms use "the prescribed time." At registration you receive a veterans preferential-treatment certificate - the later pension, employment and entrepreneurship support, and education aid all connect from there.

### 6. Three ledgers after discharge: the one-time discharge payment, service years counting as work years, and up to 20,000 yuan a year off your taxes if you work for yourself
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. At discharge the military transfers out your social-insurance relationship and funds; you then handle the continuation at the social-insurance office of the placement place - the window that handles social insurance.
- TL;DR: Three ledgers after discharge. First, the one-time discharge payment for the self-employed, paid by years of active service and increased for those with merits. Second, these service years are not a social-insurance blank: service years count as work years, and contribution periods merge with those before enlistment and after discharge. Third, taxes: if you open a shop or do business yourself, you can pay up to 20,000 yuan less tax per year for 3 years.
- Benefit: First ledger, per the Regulation on the placement of veterans: "for retired NCOs and conscripts in self-employment, a one-time discharge payment is issued according to their years of active service." Who pays: "arranged as a special item by the central budget; the specific standards are set by the State Council's veterans work authority and the Central Military Commission's political work department together with the State Council's finance department... and adjusted in due course." The increase comes in five tiers. Medal or honorary title: +25%. First-class combat merit or first-class commendation: +20%. Second-class combat merit, first-class merit or second-class commendation: +15%. Third-class combat merit or second-class merit: +10%; fourth-class combat merit or third-class merit: +5%. The regulation also states: "a veteran who does not find employment in time may claim unemployment-insurance benefits from the human-resources and social-security department of their household-registration place under law, with years of active service treated as contribution years - except veterans placed by retirement or support." Treated as contribution years means: you paid nothing in these years, and they're counted as if you had. Second ledger, per the Law on protecting veterans: "a veteran's years of active service count as work years, calculated cumulatively with the years worked at the employer after discharge." Work years means years since starting work - the service years and the post-discharge years add on continuously. The law also states that active-service years merge with pension, medical and unemployment contribution years before enlistment and after discharge "as calculated together under law," and that "the active-service experience of retired NCOs and conscripts counts as grass-roots work experience." The Military Personnel Insurance Law: "a serviceman's years of active service are treated as employee basic medical-insurance contribution years, calculated together with contribution years of employee basic medical insurance joined before enlistment and after leaving active service." Third ledger, per the 2023 Announcement No. 14 of the Ministry of Finance, State Taxation Administration and Ministry of Veterans Affairs, covering self-employed retired soldiers in individual business: "within 3 years (36 months, same below), deduct in turn from the value-added tax, urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharge, local education surcharge and individual income tax actually payable that year, up to a limit of 20,000 yuan per household per year. The limit may be raised by up to 20%." Deduct means subtracting in order from those taxes owed that year until the limit - it is not money paid out to you. For enterprises hiring them, "the fixed standard is 6,000 yuan per person per year, raisable by up to 50%." The execution period runs January 1, 2023 to December 31, 2027; those under 3 years at expiry may continue until their period completes (nationwide)

Regulation on the placement of veterans (State Council and Central Military Commission Order No. 787, Articles 21, 22, 74, 81, 85). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11526/202408/content_6969192.html>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2020). Law on protecting veterans (Articles 42, 44, 50). <http://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/fgwx/flfg/16048597.html> (published by the Ministry of National Defense); Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2012). Military Personnel Insurance Law (Articles 16, 23). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf73e160645> (national legal database); Ministry of Finance, State Taxation Administration and Ministry of Veterans Affairs (2023). Announcement on tax policy for further supporting entrepreneurship and employment of self-employed retired soldiers (Announcement No. 14 of 2023). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202308/content_6896456.htm>

So this book writes no number - and don't trust specific figures in guides either. During a conscript's service, the family can additionally receive a family preferential allowance. The Military Service Law writes only "the standard is set by local people's governments, with fixed-amount subsidies from the central budget" - ask the county-level people's government that approved your enlistment for the figure. The tax ledger is how much less tax you can owe per year, not money paid into your hand. If the business doesn't take off, the limit goes unused - hence this entry's benefit grades only "mid." The Regulation on promoting employment and entrepreneurship of veterans, effective August 1, 2026, adds one more item: skilled-personnel vocational qualification certificates earned during service are valid nationwide after discharge.

### 7. The civil-service exam isn't the only way into the establishment: two full years in a grass-roots service program qualifies you for the dedicated recruitment that takes about 10% of each year's civil-service plan
<!-- tags: money=0 time=high will=yes benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. The price is two years of service at the township grass-roots. Those two years pay a fiscal subsidy, not a market wage; the hard part is enduring them.
- TL;DR: Getting into the establishment isn't only the civil-service exam. Complete two years in a grass-roots service program like "Three Supports and One Assistance" with a passing assessment, and you can apply for the dedicated recruitment open only to such people. Each province must take about 10% of its annual civil-service recruitment plan for them. Take the graduate entrance exam within three years, and 10 points are added to your preliminary total. The price: those two years pay a fiscal subsidy.
- Benefit: The fourth-round "Three Supports and One Assistance" plan notice states "the service term is two years." It also states: "each province (autonomous region, municipality) shall take about 10% of its civil-service recruitment plan each year for dedicated recruitment targeting people from grass-roots service programs such as 'Three Supports and One Assistance.' When public institutions at the county and township grass-roots level recruit openly, they shall set aside a certain number or proportion of posts for special recruitment of people who completed 'Three Supports and One Assistance' service with a passing assessment... after hiring, no probation period need be agreed." Dedicated recruitment and special recruitment mean these places are competed for only among people who served in grass-roots programs - others can't apply. The same notice: "persons who completed 'Three Supports and One Assistance' with a passing assessment and take the national master's admissions exam within three years get 10 points added to their preliminary total and are admitted first under equal conditions... higher-vocational (junior-college) graduates who completed service with a passing assessment may enter adult higher-education junior-to-bachelor programs exam-free." Exam-free means no test - straight into the bachelor's-upgrade program. The notice also states: "persons with no work history before joining 'Three Supports and One Assistance' who complete service with a passing assessment enjoy, within two years, the same fresh-graduate policies in recruitment by organs, enterprises and public institutions, starting a business, household registration and further schooling. ### 14. If you want to work abroad, first check whether the company has a foreign labor-service cooperation qualification: it may not collect a deposit from you
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Before leaving, spend a dozen minutes verifying the company's qualification, then read both contracts through.
- TL;DR: For working abroad, the law polices not you but the company organizing your trip. Only an enterprise that has obtained the foreign labor-service cooperation qualification certificate and completed registration may do this. Article 8: "no organization or individual may organize workers to work abroad in the name of business, tourism or study abroad." Article 10: the reserve pays the service-fee refunds, labor remuneration and legally owed compensation the enterprise refuses or is unable to bear. Article 11: no organizing workers to work abroad in gambling- or pornography-related jobs. Article 12: no organizing workers to go abroad without arranged training. Article 13: the enterprise shall buy personal accident insurance for the workers' time working abroad. Article 26: within 10 working days of the contract's conclusion, it is filed with the commerce authority. Article 29: where the worker's actual rights and interests don't match the contract, the enterprise shall assist in claiming; where due compensation isn't obtained, the worker has the right to demand the enterprise bear compensation liability. Article 31: where the security situation of the country or region can't guarantee personal safety, the enterprise may not organize workers to go there. Article 37: the commerce authority builds a bad-credit-record and announcement system. The penalties follow. Article 39: engaging in foreign labor-service cooperation without qualification is investigated and banned; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability follows. Article 40: organizing trips abroad in the name of business, tourism or study, lending out the qualification, or organizing gambling- or pornography-related work gets the qualification certificate revoked. - Notes: This entry is about how the legitimate route works. Projects sending over 100 people to the same country or region must arrange accompanying management staff and file with the embassy or consulate. Smaller projects lack this layer - keep your own contract and transfer records all the more carefully.

### 15. Working remotely for a foreign company from home: this money counts as China-sourced income for tax - you file your own individual income tax, and the money comes in as "employee remuneration"
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. One annual reconciliation filing per year, from March 1 to June 30 of the following year. When incoming remittances exceed the annual quota, you also prepare an employment contract and income proof. The hard part is remembering to file this tax yourself.
- TL;DR: Whether this money counts as domestic or foreign income looks at where you do the work, not where the money is sent from. Working remotely for a foreign company while physically in China counts as China-sourced income, taxed in China. The foreign company has no establishment in China and no one withholds for you - you must file this tax yourself. The money comes in through the "employee remuneration" route.
- Benefit: Whether this money counts as China-sourced, per Article 3 of the Regulation on implementing the Individual Income Tax Law (State Council Order No. 707, effective January 1, 2019): "except as otherwise provided by the finance and tax authorities under the State Council, the following income is China-sourced regardless of where it is paid: (1) income from providing labor services within China by reason of employment, hire or contract performance." Article 6 of the same regulation defines two tax categories. One is wages and salaries: "wage and salary income means wages, salaries, bonuses, year-end raises, labor dividends, allowances, subsidies and other income related to employment or hire obtained by an individual by reason of employment or hire." The other is labor remuneration: "labor-remuneration income means income obtained by an individual from providing labor services, including design, decoration, installation, drafting, testing, laboratory work, medical treatment, law, accounting, consulting, lecturing, translation, manuscript review, calligraphy and painting, sculpture, film and television, audio recording, video recording, performance, acting, advertising, exhibition, technical services, introduction services, brokerage services, agency services and other labor services." Article 6 of the Individual Income Tax Law on computing taxable income: "for a resident individual's comprehensive income, the taxable income is the balance after deducting 60,000 yuan of expenses, special deductions, special additional deductions and other lawfully determined deductions from the annual income amount." The same article states "labor-remuneration income, author's remuneration and royalty income take as the income amount the balance after deducting 20% of the income as expenses." That 20% deduction is listed only for these three items - wages and salaries are not in it. In other words, wages and salaries count at full amount into the income figure, and the two categories compute different taxes. Article 9: "for individual income tax, the income earner is the taxpayer, and the unit or individual paying the income is the withholding agent." Article 10: "a taxpayer shall file a tax return under law in any of these situations: ... (2) obtaining taxable income with no withholding agent; ... (4) obtaining foreign-sourced income." Article 11: "a resident individual's comprehensive income is computed annually... where a reconciliation filing is needed, it shall be done between March 1 and June 30 of the year following the income." Money coming in, per the Measures for the administration of individual foreign exchange (People's Bank of China Order [2006] No. 3, effective February 1, 2007). Article 9: "individuals' foreign-exchange settlement and domestic individuals' foreign-exchange purchase are subject to annual quota management. Within the annual quota, the transaction is done at the bank on the person's valid ID; beyond the annual quota, current-account transactions are done at the bank on the person's valid ID plus transaction-amount proof and other materials." Article 7: "banks and individuals handling individual foreign-exchange business shall comply with these measures, and may not evade quota supervision by splitting transactions or use false commercial documents or vouchers to evade authenticity checks." Article 2 of the Implementation rules for the Measures for the administration of individual foreign exchange (Huifa [2007] No. 1): "individuals' foreign-exchange settlement and domestic individuals' foreign-exchange purchase are subject to annual quota management. The annual quotas are the equivalent of USD 50,000 per person per year each." Article 10: "domestic individuals' non-business current-account settlement beyond the annual quota is done at the bank on the person's valid ID plus the following proof materials: ... (7) employee remuneration: employment contract and income proof" (nationwide)

- Notes: Which tax category applies depends on your relationship with the counterparty. An employment contract with work arranged by them: wages and salaries. Taking projects yourself and delivering results: labor remuneration. The two compute different taxes - work out which you are before filing. ### 16. Starting on your own without an employer: look at the entrepreneurship guarantee loan first - up to 300,000 yuan for individuals, with the fisc covering half the interest
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Consulting is free. First make one trip to the human-resources and social-security department for qualification review, then one procedure each at the guarantee institution and the handling bank - the handling bank is the one that issues this loan. The hard part is that borrowed money must be repaid in the end.
- TL;DR: If you must borrow to start, look at this fiscally subsidized one first. An individual can borrow up to 300,000 yuan for up to 3 years, with the fisc paying half the interest. One hard condition is often missed: at application, neither you nor your spouse may have other loans - only student loans, poverty-relief microcredit, mortgages, car loans and small consumer loans under 50,000 yuan don't count.
- Benefit: The individual application conditions are written in Article 6 of the Measures for the administration of special inclusive-finance development funds (Caijin [2023] No. 75). First, you must belong to a key employment group. The document lists: registered urban unemployed, people with employment difficulties (including people with disabilities), veterans, and people released after serving a sentence. Plus college graduates (including college-graduate village officials and returned overseas students), employees and unemployed people of enterprises resolving excess capacity. Plus returned-home migrant workers starting businesses, online merchants, people lifted out of poverty, and rural residents starting businesses independently. Second, no other loans in your name: "other than student loans, poverty-relief microcredit, housing loans, vehicle-purchase loans and small consumer loans under 50,000 yuan (including credit-card spending), the applicant and their spouse have no other loans when submitting the entrepreneurship guarantee loan application." Article 9: "for qualifying entrepreneurship guarantee loans, the finance department provides a fiscal interest subsidy of 50% of the loan's actual rate. For extended or overdue entrepreneurship guarantee loans, the finance department in principle provides no subsidy." The subsidy means the fisc pays half the interest. Extension means pushing the maturity back; overdue means past due unpaid - in both cases the fisc stops paying. Article 10: for individual entrepreneurship guarantee loans under 100,000 yuan, the counter-guarantee is in principle waived - no need to find another person or put up property to guarantee the guarantee institution. Article 11: individual entrepreneurship guarantee loans in the award-subsidy support scope are "capped at 300,000 yuan, with a term of no more than 3 years." Partnerships may go moderately higher, at most 110% of the sum of the individual caps. Award-supported loan rate caps come in three tiers: LPR+50BPs in the eastern region, LPR+150BPs in the central and western, LPR+250BPs in poverty-relief areas. LPR is the banks' loan prime rate; BPs is the unit it's measured in - the more added, the higher the interest. Those with "active repayment, strong job creation and a good project" may apply again after maturity, up to 3 times in total. Article 8: the applicant first applies to the local human-resources and social-security department for qualification review, then submits the application to the guarantee institution and the handling bank. Article 12: with the provincial people's government's consent, localities may expand the support scope and raise the caps and subsidy ratios (nationwide)
- Evidence: A

- Notes: The fisc paying half your interest doesn't change "borrowed money must be repaid."
