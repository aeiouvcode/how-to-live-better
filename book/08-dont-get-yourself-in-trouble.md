# 8. Don't Get Yourself in Trouble: Law and Asset Safety

This chapter counts money and personal freedom - nothing is converted into lifespan. It makes three things clear: what responsibility the law assigns you, how much less you pay by doing it right, and what procedural rights you hold when something happens. Article numbers have been checked against the official texts. Ordering: the biggest one-time losses that are also the cheapest to prevent come first; what needs arranging in advance sits in the middle; experience-only entries without official documents come last. This chapter is China-specific: statutes, hotlines and agencies named are China's.

### 1. After a traffic accident, stop, help the injured, call the police - don't run
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Stopping, turning on the hazard lights, helping the injured, and calling the police costs at most an hour or two waiting for traffic police. For a minor accident with no injuries and no dispute over the facts, take photos first, then note the other party's name, phone, plate number, driver's-license number, insurance-policy number and the point of impact. After both sides sign, you can leave the scene and negotiate yourselves.
- TL;DR: Run, and the accident defaults to your full responsibility. Your license is revoked and you can never test again. More practical still: vehicle-damage and third-party insurance never pay for hit-and-run - hundreds of thousands of yuan in damages come out of your own pocket. Staying to help and report costs an hour or two at most.
- Benefit: The party who runs bears full responsibility for the accident; only if you prove the other side was also at fault can responsibility shrink a little. Running also revokes your driver's license, for life. If the accident constitutes the crime of causing traffic casualties, running brings 3 to 7 years; if running causes a death, 7 years or more. Insurance won't help either: in commercial auto insurance, vehicle-damage and third-party coverage never pay for "hit-and-run" or "deliberately destroying, fabricating the scene, or destroying evidence" - hundreds of thousands of yuan come out of your own pocket (nationwide)
- Evidence: A

Road Traffic Safety Law (2021 revision, Articles 70, 101). <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html> (reposted by Beijing Traffic Management Bureau) ; State Council (2017). Implementation Regulation for the Road Traffic Safety Law (State Council Order No. 405, 2017 revision, Articles 86, 92). <http://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=75> ; National People's Congress (1997). Model clauses for commercial motor vehicle insurance (2020 edition, Articles 9, 22). <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Notes: If someone is hurt, help them first, then report. If helping requires moving the scene, mark the original positions of vehicles and people first. "I didn't feel the bump and drove on" can still be counted as fleeing afterward - when unsure, report. The insurance part quotes the model clauses - the industry's unified template; what you actually get paid follows your own policy.

### 2. When you realize you've been scammed, call 110 or 96110 immediately and demand a payment stop - don't investigate on your own first
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. One phone call, stating the transfer time, amount and the recipient's account. Keep the transfer screenshots and chat records.
- TL;DR: The short stretch right after money leaves your account is the only window. During it, police can stop the payment urgently and freeze fast, and banks must cooperate. Once the money is layered away and scattered, it's basically unrecoverable. So call 110 or 96110 first - don't go investigate or ask around yourself first. An incoming call from 96110 is the police trying to talk you out of something - answer it.
- Benefit: Public security organs have the power to run instant queries, urgent payment stops and fast freezes on funds involved in a case. Banks and payment institutions must cooperate. Frozen money is returned to victims through procedure. Once money is transferred onward and scattered, the chance of recovery drops sharply - so the earlier the stop, the better. 96110 is the national unified anti-fraud warning hotline; an incoming call is the police dissuading you - answer it (nationwide, from December 2022)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law (Articles 20, 34). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml> ; Fujian Provincial Public Security Department (2023). When 96110 calls, please answer. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm> (provincial police page)
- Notes: Anyone claiming to be police, a prosecutor or a court who tells you to move money to a "safe account" - it's a scam. The law only writes "establish a payment-stop and freeze system", meaning the bank holds the money. How much is recovered depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report. The "National Anti-Fraud Center" app and 96110 descriptions are quoted from the Fujian police page; the Ministry of Public Security site's original page won't open.

### 3. Remember the hard anti-fraud rules: don't trust incoming calls, don't disclose information, don't click links, verify before transferring - the seven most common scams all have this shape
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Memorize the "three don'ts and one do", read through the seven categories below, install the "National Anti-Fraud Center" app, and always answer 96110. Anything that asks you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unfamiliar app, click a link, or read out a verification code - hang up first, then verify through the official number. The hard part: the other side usually presses hard, and you have to hold against the pressure and hang up first.
- TL;DR: Anything that asks you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unfamiliar app, click a link, or read out a verification code - hang up first, then call back through the official number to verify. The seven high-frequency types: order-brushing rebates, fake investment and wealth management, online loans, fake customer service, fake police-prosecutor-court, pig-butchering, and lust-bait order-brushing. They share exactly one thing: getting you to transfer money, pay a fee, or share your screen first. Recognizing it beforehand is far cheaper than chasing money afterward.
- Benefit: The law requires governments and relevant departments to explain the methods of telecom and online fraud to the public. Police, together with financial, telecom and cyberspace departments, run a warning-and-dissuasion system that promptly dissuades people about to be scammed. 96110 is that national unified hotline - answer its calls. The Fujian police's type-by-type recognition features: Order-brushing rebates: "someone inviting you to make money together most likely wants to make money off you"; unfinished task orders mean your principal isn't returned. Fake investment and wealth management: shills in the group post profits; small rebates come first, then you're pushed to invest more, and withdrawing requires "paying tax". Online loans: "a loan never requires a deposit or fabricated bank statements - if you meet one, it's fake, always". Fake customer service: claims you opened a membership that will auto-charge and hurt your credit record, pressuring you to transfer to cancel. Fake police-prosecutor-court: "there is no such thing as a 'safe account'; any 'police, prosecutor or court' handling a case or taking a statement by phone, QQ, WeChat or internet is fraud". Pig-butchering: "a 'lover' you have never met, seen only in photos or videos, cannot be trusted" - never video-calls, never meets, but often has emergencies needing money. Lust-bait order-brushing: meeting requires "doing tasks" first, and the payments grow. Recognizing beforehand is far cheaper than a payment stop afterward; how much a stop recovers depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report - how to stop is in entry 2 of this chapter (call 110 or 96110 and demand a payment stop) (nationwide, from December 2022)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law (Articles 8, 20, 34). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml> ; Fujian Provincial Public Security Department (2023). When 96110 calls, please answer. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm> (provincial police page, with the "don't trust incoming calls, don't disclose information, don't click links, verify before transferring" mnemonic and the "beware scam calls starting with 192" reminder) ; Fujian Provincial Public Security Department (2023-2024). Scam-spotting column: order-brushing <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202307/t20230703_6196881.htm> ; investment and wealth management <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202312/t20231220_6362145.htm> ; loans <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202312/t20231207_6326741.htm> ; fake customer service <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202310/t20231020_6279505.htm> ; fake police <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202309/t20230913_6255863.htm> ; pig-butchering <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202308/t20230818_6232030.htm> ; lust-bait <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202403/t20240307_6410612.htm>
- Notes: Claimed police, customer service, leaders, or platform risk-control asking you to "cooperate with an investigation", "cancel a membership", "brush orders for rebates" or "invest on inside information" are the same scheme in different skins. A real loan never requires a deposit; the authorities have no "safe account" - remembering those two sentences blocks more than half. Grade B because the mnemonic is official publicity, without a number for "how much less fraud follows from remembering it". The MPS website (mps.gov.cn) wouldn't open during this verification; the mnemonic and the seven-type breakdown are taken from a provincial police page. ### 4. A face on video or a voice on the phone is not verification - if money transfer comes up, hang up and call back on the old number in your own contacts
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. One extra phone call, a few extra minutes. Normally, post fewer full-face videos and long voice clips on public accounts. The hard part: the other side often presses hard, and you have to resist transferring on the spot.
- TL;DR: Seeing the face and hearing the voice both fail as verification. The moment a transfer comes up, hang up, then call back on the old number saved in your own contacts, or ask a mutual acquaintance. If the other side claims "bad signal" or "in a meeting" and won't video live, treat it as a scam.
- Benefit: The MPS cybersecurity bureau gives three methods. First, motion verification: ask the other side to turn their head in one continuous motion, or cover the face fully with a palm and remove it quickly. The original text: "current AI technology, when simulating these complex facial occlusions and lighting changes, is prone to stutter, blur or distortion". Second, listen to voice details: "AI-generated speech sometimes lacks natural breathing pauses and emotional range, or carries a slight mechanical quality and abnormal background noise". Third, multi-channel verification. The original text calls this "the single most core move": "after hanging up, directly dial the number the person usually uses and you know well (do not redial the suspicious incoming number), or verify sideways through a third person you both know". The mnemonic: "don't lightly trust video or voice; verify with motion and more". On the legal side, face-swapping and voice-swapping have long been banned for fraud. Face generation, face replacement, synthesized voices and voice imitation services must carry a prominent label from the platform. No organization or individual may maliciously delete, tamper with, forge or hide that label. But law-abiding platforms are the ones that label - scammers won't - so "no AI label visible" cannot be read backward as proof the other side is a real person. The MPS criminal-investigation bureau published a case: a 14-person ring was caught with over 50 thousand synthesized dynamic face videos and over 500 thousand items of citizens' personal information; police also seized 60 cloud servers (nationwide, labeling duty from September 1, 2025)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: MPS Cybersecurity Bureau (2025). Seeing is not believing - beware new AI fraud! <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202509/t20250922_7013008.htm> (reposted by Fujian police) ; Cyberspace Administration of China, MIIT, MPS (2022). Rules on deep synthesis in internet information services (Articles 6, 16, 17, effective January 10, 2023). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-12/12/content_5731431.htm> ; CAC, MIIT, MPS, NRTA (2025). Measures for labeling AI-generated and synthesized content (Article 10, effective September 1, 2025). <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm> ; MPS Criminal Investigation Bureau and CCTV News (2026). Police seize over 50,000 synthesized dynamic face videos - who is "copying" your face? <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202603/t20260330_7117146.htm> (reposted by Fujian police)
- Notes: Call back on the old number saved in your own contacts. Don't redial the number that showed on the incoming call, and don't use a new number the other side sends in chat. If they won't video live or won't do the specified motion, citing "bad signal" or "in a meeting", treat it as a scam. Motion verification is only an aid - AI keeps improving; only the third method, verification, holds. If you've already transferred money, see entry 2 of this chapter (call 110 immediately and demand a payment stop). Preventing your own face and voice from being harvested: chapter 14. Grade B because officials give recognition methods and cases, not a number for "how much the scam rate drops after verifying this way". The MPS website (mps.gov.cn) again returned site-wide 521 errors during verification; the MPS bureau originals are taken from Fujian police reposts.

### 5. If accused or summoned, get a lawyer first - no private settlement, no deleting records
<!-- tags: money=high time=mid will=some benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Lawyer fees start in the thousands of yuan. Those in financial hardship can apply to a legal aid agency for a free lawyer. When questioned, answer only questions related to the case and leave the rest to the lawyer. The hard part is staying composed on the spot and not rushing to explain yourself.
- TL;DR: From the very first questioning, you may retain a lawyer. When the lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center arranges it within 48 hours at the latest, and the conversation isn't monitored. Questions unrelated to the case, you may refuse to answer.
- Benefit: From the first interrogation, or from when a compulsory measure is taken against you (your personal freedom restricted), you have the right to retain a lawyer. During the investigation stage while the case is with police, only a lawyer may act as defender - no one else. When a lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center arranges it within 48 hours at the latest; the meeting is not monitored. No one may be forced to prove their own guilt. Questions unrelated to the case, you have the right to refuse. A summons or compelled appearance lasts at most 12 hours at a time, 24 hours for especially major and complex cases. A summons notifies you to appear for questioning; compelled appearance brings you by force. Consecutive summonses may not be used to detain someone in disguise. Fabricating facts to frame another person, intending to have them criminally prosecuted: up to 3 years, 3 to 10 if serious consequences follow. If after detention or arrest the case is withdrawn, not prosecuted, or ends in acquittal, you can claim state compensation, counted per day at the previous year's national average daily wage of employees (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2018). Criminal Procedure Law (2018 amendment, Articles 34, 35, 39, 52, 119, 120). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml> ; National People's Congress (1997). Criminal Law (Article 243). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml> ; NPC Standing Committee (2012). State Compensation Law (2012 amendment, Articles 17, 33). <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html> (reposted by the National Bureau of Statistics)
- Notes: Don't approach the other party privately, and don't approach their family. Any "private settlement" can be read as an admission. Chat records, call records and travel records are your materials for proving innocence - deleting them deletes your own evidence. Misremembering, or a report that later doesn't hold up, is not the crime of false accusation. This entry applies to the accused regardless of sex. The expanded three entries: entries 33, 34 and 35 of this chapter (pursuing the fabricating party; acquittal when evidence is insufficient, with appeal and retrial; state compensation counted per day after custody).

### 6. When something happens, go explain it yourself: voluntary surrender can cut a sentence by up to 40%, and the "wait out the limitation period" road is blocked
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Go to the police yourself to surrender, or stay where you are and call to report, then recount the matter truthfully. The hard part is resisting the thought "wait a bit longer, maybe nobody will investigate".
- TL;DR: Voluntary surrender can cut a sentence by up to 40%; for minor circumstances punishment can be waived outright. Truthful confession only after being found still cuts up to 20%. Fleeing or refusing to admit buys nothing. Hoping to hide a few years and let the case expire doesn't work either: once a case is filed, the time you spend hiding doesn't count toward the limitation period.
- Benefit: Criminal Law Article 67: after a crime, voluntarily giving yourself up and truthfully confessing is voluntary surrender. For voluntary surrender, punishment may be lightened or mitigated; for a minor crime it may be waived. Without surrender but with truthful confession of your crime (candor), punishment may be lightened; when truthful confession avoids especially serious consequences, it may be mitigated. The two supreme organs' sentencing guidance gives explicit ranges. The benchmark sentence is the term first computed from the case facts; the percentages below reduce it. Voluntary surrender can cut the benchmark by up to 40%; for a minor crime, more than 40% or a lawful waiver. Within candor: truthful confession of your own crime cuts up to 20%; confessing a graver crime of the same kind not yet known to the authorities cuts 10% to 30%; avoiding especially serious consequences through confession cuts 30% to 50%. Pleading guilty and accepting punishment cuts up to 30%; combined with surrender, major candor, returning ill-gotten gains, or compensation and forgiveness, up to 60%; for a minor crime, over 60% or a lawful waiver. On the other side, the limitation period comes in four tiers by the statutory maximum sentence: 5, 10, 15, 20 years (Article 87). But Article 88 states: once the procuratorate, police or state-security organ has opened an investigation, or a court has accepted the case, evading investigation or trial frees the case from the limitation period (nationwide, sentencing ranges effective July 1, 2021)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: National People's Congress. Criminal Law of the PRC (consolidated text through Amendment XI), Articles 67, 87, 88. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181796a636a0179822a19640c92> ; Supreme People's Court and Supreme People's Procuratorate (2021). Sentencing guidance for common crimes (trial) (Fa Fa [2021] No. 21, effective July 1, 2021), parts 3(6)(7)(14). Full text reposted by Zhangye People's Procuratorate, Gansu. <http://www.zhangye.jcy.gov.cn/info/1039/2086.htm> ; Supreme People's Court (2010). Opinions on specific questions in handling voluntary surrender and meritorious service (Fa Fa [2010] No. 60), part 1. SPC Gazette. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/f3089058d37e11cd16d801530934ae.html>
- Notes: This entry's beneficiary is yourself. Voluntary surrender is a leniency circumstance, not a get-out-of-jail card - how much is cut is the court's call, and the sentencing opinion demands tight control of leniency for serious violent and drug crimes. Traffic accidents have their own arithmetic: staying at the scene, helping the injured and reporting to police counts as voluntarily giving yourself up, but because those are statutory duties to begin with, the leniency range is tightly controlled; surrendering after fleeing still counts as surrender, only the heavier statutory tier becomes the benchmark before deciding whether and how much to cut - what to do is in entry 1 of this chapter (stop, help, report). Getting a lawyer first and confessing truthfully don't conflict - entry 5 of this chapter (get a lawyer first): the lawyer checks that the transcript is written correctly, not help you invent a story.

### 7. After drinking, don't touch the wheel - including e-bikes and "just moving the car"
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=some benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: A designated-driver service or taxi costs tens of yuan. Before the gathering, settle who isn't drinking today. The hard part is pushing back when the table presses you to drink.
- TL;DR: Driving after drinking: license suspended 6 months, fined one to two thousand yuan. Repeat: detention plus revocation. Drunk driving: license revoked outright, no retest for 5 years, plus prosecution for dangerous driving - a criminal record that affects political vetting and some professions. Insurance won't help either: driving after drinking, auto insurance never pays. "Just moving the car a bit" still counts as driving.
- Benefit: Driving after drinking: license suspended 6 months, fined 1,000 to 2,000 yuan. Repeat offense: up to 10 days' detention and license revocation. Drunk driving revokes the license outright, no retest for 5 years, and criminal responsibility for dangerous driving - criminal detention plus a fine, detention meaning short-term custody. Commercial vehicles are heavier: drunk-driving one brings 15 days' detention, a 5,000 yuan fine and a 5-year driving ban; drunk-driving a commercial vehicle brings a 10-year ban and a lifetime ban on commercial driving. Causing a major accident that constitutes a crime after drinking or drunk driving: lifetime ban. Commercial auto insurance never pays for driving after drinking or drug use (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2021). Road Traffic Safety Law (2021 revision, Article 91). <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html> (reposted by Beijing Traffic Management Bureau) ; NPC Standing Committee (2015). Criminal Law Amendment (IX) (Article 8, i.e. Criminal Law Article 133a). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml> ; China Insurance Association (2020). Model clauses for commercial motor vehicle insurance (2020 edition, Articles 9, 22). <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Notes: Drunk driving is a crime and leaves a record, affecting your own political vetting and some professional qualifications. "Drinking" and "drunk" are drawn by blood alcohol content under a national standard. This chapter hasn't verified those values - don't estimate by feel which tier you've reached.

### 8. Lend your bank card, SIM card, or payment account to no one - "point-running" is not a side job
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. When someone says "lend me your card to route some money, I'll give you a few hundred", just refuse.
- TL;DR: Renting, lending or selling bank cards, SIM cards and payment accounts is itself illegal. The fine is 1 to 10 times the illegal gains; with no gains, up to 200,000 yuan; serious cases bring 15 days' detention, plus credit-record marks and restricted account functions. If you know the other side is using it for crime and still help receive and move money, that's the crime of aiding information-network crime - up to 3 years.
- Benefit: Renting, lending, buying or selling phone cards, bank accounts, payment accounts and internet accounts is itself illegal. Illegal gains are confiscated, plus a fine of 1 to 10 times the gains; with no gains, up to 200,000 yuan. Serious circumstances: up to 15 days' detention. You may also be marked in credit records, have account functions restricted, and lose non-counter services - everything except in-person counter business stops. Knowing another person is using the network to commit crime and still providing help like payment settlement, where circumstances are serious, constitutes the crime of aiding information-network criminal activity ("aiding-network crime") - up to 3 years plus a fine (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law (Articles 31, 44). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml> ; NPC Standing Committee (2015). Criminal Law Amendment (IX) (Article 29, i.e. Criminal Law Article 287a). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>
- Notes: "I didn't know what he'd do with the card" doesn't block it. Case handling infers your knowledge from external circumstances: taking a fee, obviously abnormal transaction flows - either can count as knowing. If your card is used to receive criminal proceeds, even if you end up not a criminal, the account gets frozen and you get disciplined.

### 9. Check your own credit report free twice a year - look for loans and cards you never opened
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Check online - ten minutes does it.
- TL;DR: Regulation gives you the right to pull your own credit report free twice a year - ten minutes online. Adverse records stay 5 years from the day the matter ends; beyond 5 years they should be deleted. Dispute a report and a written answer must come within 20 days. Use the People's Bank credit-center site or mobile banking to check - not third-party "check your credit" apps, which are themselves harvesting your information.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation writes "a personal information subject has the right to obtain their own credit report free twice a year" - two free self-checks a year. Adverse information is kept "for 5 years from the day the adverse act or event ends; beyond 5 years it shall be deleted". If you dispute the report, the credit bureau or the institution that supplied the entry must answer you in writing within 20 days of receiving the objection.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2013). Regulation on the Credit Reporting Industry (State Council Order No. 631, Articles 16, 17, 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-01/29/content_2303.htm>
- Notes: When someone uses your stolen ID for online loans or cards, the victim usually finds out only when a mortgage is rejected - years later by then. The entrances are the PBOC Credit Reference Center website or mobile banking. Don't use third-party "check your credit" apps - those apps are themselves harvesting your information.

### 10. When a conflict flares, call the police and don't swing - the one who swings first almost always loses
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Back off to somewhere crowded and call the police; let the other side's moves stay on camera. The hard part is not talking back and not shoving.
- TL;DR: Hitting someone: 5 to 10 days' detention, fined 500 to 1,000 yuan. Gang beating, hitting the elderly, pregnant, children or disabled, or repeated beating: 10 to 15 days, fined one to two thousand. Cause light injury and it's a criminal case - up to 3 years; serious injury, 3 to 10. Two people fighting each other is not self-defense, and the one who swung first almost always loses. Backing off to somewhere crowded and calling the police works better than hitting back.
- Benefit: Beating another person or deliberately injuring: 5 to 10 days' detention plus a 500 to 1,000 yuan fine. Gang beating; beating the elderly, pregnant women, children or people with disabilities; repeated beatings or beating multiple people at once: 10 to 15 days plus 1,000 to 2,000 yuan. Group brawling is handled as picking quarrels and provoking trouble - making trouble out of nothing, stirring disorder - up to 15 days' detention plus 2,000 yuan. Deliberately injuring another's body where it constitutes a crime: up to 3 years; causing serious injury, 3 to 10. Two people fighting each other - legally mutual affray - is not self-defense. But if the other side struck first with clearly excessive means, or you have tried hard to get away and they keep coming, your hitting back is generally recognized as defense. Only hitting back too far counts as excessive defense, and that takes both conditions at once: clearly exceeding necessary limits, and causing major harm. Even truly excessive defense should get mitigated or waived punishment (nationwide, Public Security Administration Punishments Law effective January 1, 2026)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2025). Public Security Administration Punishments Law (2025 revision, Articles 30, 51). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml> ; National People's Congress (1997). Criminal Law (Articles 20, 234). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml> ; Supreme People's Court, Supreme People's Procuratorate, Ministry of Public Security (2020). Guidance on lawfully applying the self-defense system (Fa Fa [2020] No. 31, items 6, 9, 11, 14). <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/251611.html>
- Notes: After the 2025 revision of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law, every article number changed - beating moved from old Article 43 to Article 51; materials citing old numbers are outdated. What counts as the "light injury" in "light injury means a sentence" is set by a forensic standard this chapter hasn't verified. Once the other side is down or has fled, the infringement has stopped; chasing them down to keep hitting is not defense.

### 11. When harm can't be escaped you may fight back - but only against the person attacking, and you stop when they stop
<!-- tags: money=high time=mid will=some benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Lawyer fees start in the thousands of yuan; those in hardship can apply for legal aid. The price is mostly afterward: cases often open first as intentional injury, you cooperate with the investigation, and you may even be held for ten-plus days first. The hard part is staying steady through those days.
- TL;DR: To stop ongoing unlawful harm to yourself or another, acting to stop it and injuring the attacker carries no criminal responsibility. ### 33. If someone fabricates facts to report you, you can demand they be pursued: public-security level means 5 days' detention up, criminal level up to 3 years
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=mid lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Report to the police, explaining the other side fabricated it. The hard part is producing things that prove they made it up deliberately.
- TL;DR: Fabricating facts to falsely accuse someone, intending to get them criminally prosecuted or public-security punished, is itself illegal. It brings up to 5 days' detention or up to 1,000 yuan; graver, 5 to 10 days. At criminal level it's the crime of false accusation - up to 3 years. But misremembering or an erroneous report doesn't count - to have them pursued, you must prove the falsehood was deliberate.
- Benefit: The Public Security Administration Punishments Law (revised June 27, 2025, effective January 1, 2026), Article 50, covers false accusation: fabricating facts to falsely accuse another person, attempting to have them criminally prosecuted or public-security punished, is punishable - up to five days' detention or up to 1,000 yuan; graver circumstances, five to ten days plus a possible fine up to 1,000 yuan. Criminal Law Article 243 covers the crime of false accusation: fabricating facts to falsely accuse another person with intent to have them criminally prosecuted, where circumstances are serious - up to three years' imprisonment, detention or public surveillance; where serious consequences result, three to ten years. State-organ personnel committing this crime are punished heavily - "heavily" meaning toward the heavy end within the same tier. Not deliberate falsehood but an erroneous report or an inaccurate accusation doesn't fall under those paragraphs - misremembering and mistaken reports aren't pursued under them (nationwide)

### 34. Insufficient evidence should mean acquittal by design, confessions extracted under coercion must be excluded, and after a sentence there remain appeal and retrial
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: The procedures themselves are free, but appeals and retrials eat time. A lawyer is usually needed, from thousands of yuan; those in hardship can apply for legal aid - entry 5 of this chapter (get a lawyer first). The hard part is enduring round after round without giving up.
- TL;DR: The conviction threshold is written into the Criminal Procedure Law: evidence supporting a verdict must be verified as true through legal procedure, and all evidence combined must exclude reasonable doubt. Failing that, acquittal should follow. Confessions extracted by torture, and testimony taken through violence or threats, must be excluded and cannot ground a judgment. After a judgment takes effect, you, your statutory agent and your close relatives can still petition.
- Benefit: Criminal Procedure Law Article 55: evidence being reliable and sufficient requires these conditions - all evidence supporting the verdict has been verified as true through legal procedure, and all evidence combined excludes reasonable doubt about the facts found. Excluding reasonable doubt means that viewing the whole case's evidence together, the facts found leave no other plausible possibility standing. Article 56: confessions of suspects and defendants collected through torture or other illegal methods, and witness testimony and victim statements collected through violence, threats or other illegal methods, shall be excluded. During investigation, prosecution review and trial, evidence that should be excluded shall be excluded by law and may not ground a prosecution recommendation, a prosecution decision or a judgment. Article 200(3): where evidence is insufficient and the defendant cannot be found guilty, an acquittal shall be rendered on insufficient evidence with the charged crime not established. Article 252 gives the petition route: a party, their statutory agent and close relatives may petition a people's court or procuratorate against a legally effective judgment or ruling - but a petition does not stop the judgment's execution. Petitioning is petitioning; the original judgment still executes. Article 253 lists the circumstances under which a people's court shall retry (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2018). Criminal Procedure Law (2018 amendment, Articles 55, 56, 200, 252, 253). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>
- Notes: These are the system's written bottom lines, not a guarantee of outcome. This book has no verifiable official figures for acquittal rates or petition-reversal rates in Chinese criminal cases, so no proportions are written and no individual case is judged. Three things you can do yourself. First, retain a lawyer after the first interrogation - entry 5 of this chapter (get a lawyer first). Second, read the transcript word by word before signing. Third, don't admit to things you didn't do to "get out sooner" - a guilty plea with acceptance of punishment is a leniency circumstance, not an exemption. Where the statutory circumstances fit, the court shall retry.

### 35. If the case is withdrawn, not prosecuted, or ends in acquittal after custody, apply for state compensation - counted by the day
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. File the compensation application with the organ that ordered the detention, arrest, prosecution or judgment.
- TL;DR: Detained or arrested, then case withdrawn, not prosecuted, or acquitted - you can apply for state compensation. The personal-freedom item is counted per day: from May 18, 2026, 495.94 yuan a day - a year in custody is about 180,000 yuan. You can also demand that the impact be eliminated, your reputation restored, and an apology made; serious consequences bring additional solatium for emotional harm. One case the state won't pay: you deliberately gave a false confession or fabricated incriminating evidence that got you jailed.
- Benefit: State Compensation Law Article 17 lists the circumstances where a victim has the right to compensation. First, detention: detention measures taken in violation of the Criminal Procedure Law, or lawful detention exceeding the statutory time limit - followed by withdrawal of the case, non-prosecution, or an acquittal ending criminal pursuit. Second, arrest: arrest followed by withdrawal, non-prosecution or acquittal ending criminal pursuit. Third, retrial: a case retried under the trial-supervision procedure with the verdict changed to not guilty, where the original sentence has already been served. Trial-supervision is the procedure for pulling an effective judgment back up for review. Article 33: for infringement of personal freedom, the daily compensation is computed at the state's previous-year average daily wage of employees. Article 35: where emotional harm is caused, the organ shall eliminate the impact, restore the reputation and make an apology within the scope of the infringement's effect; where serious consequences result, corresponding solatium shall be paid. Article 19: where a citizen was held or sentenced because of their own deliberate false confession or fabrication of other incriminating evidence, the state bears no compensation liability. The Supreme People's Procuratorate issued a notice in May 2026: the National Bureau of Statistics announced on May 15, 2026 that the 2025 national average annual wage of urban non-private-sector employees was 129,441 yuan, giving an average daily wage of 495.94 yuan. From May 18, 2026, procuratorates at all levels compute compensation for infringing personal freedom at 495.94 yuan per day. The previous standard, 475.52 yuan a day, applied from May 2025 (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2012 amendment). State Compensation Law (Articles 17, 19, 33, 35). <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html> (reposted by the National Bureau of Statistics) ; Supreme People's Procuratorate (2026). - Notes: This per-day standard updates every May with the previous year's urban non-private-sector average annual wage from the statistics bureau - check the current year's figure when using it, don't copy this entry's. Who pays is the organ that decided to hold you - police, procuratorate or court; file with it first, and if you disagree with its handling, apply upward through the State Compensation Law's procedure. ### 36. Claiming compensation as the victim yourself: go through 12315, a lawsuit or a lawyer - don't meet the other side alone, and never bind "pay me" and "I won't expose you" into one sentence
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Claim through 12315, sue in court, or have a lawyer make the demand. Record the whole of any negotiation meeting. The hard part is resisting negotiating alone with the other side.
- TL;DR: Being the victim and claiming compensation from the infringing party has legal basis in itself - a "high ask" doesn't automatically turn it into extortion. But the price can be huge: Guo Li was first sentenced to five years for extortion and only later acquitted on retrial. So use channels that leave a trail, don't go alone to a place the other side picks, and never speak "pay me" and "I won't expose you" as one condition.
- Benefit: The official case: in 2008, after authorities announced that some batches of "Scient" brand milk powder contained melamine, Guo Li's daughter's test showed "several punctate strong echoes visible in the central collecting systems of both kidneys". ### 37. If you're cyberbullied: turn on protection and preserve evidence first, then pick among the platform, an injunction, and the police

### 39. When you report to police, demand the case-acceptance receipt on the spot; a refusal to file comes with written notice: 7 days to request reconsideration, 7 more for review, and the procuratorate can order the filing

- TL;DR: Reporting a case and filing a case are two things. When police accept a case they should give you a case-acceptance receipt - the only proof that you "reported" - demand it on the spot. A decision not to file should come with a written non-filing notice. Disagree, and within 7 days you can apply for reconsideration; disagree with that, and within 7 days apply for review to the level above. You can also take it to the procuratorate and ask it to supervise the filing.
- Benefit: The Rules on Public Security Organs' Handling of Criminal Cases (as amended by MPS Order No. 159), Article 171, covers the acceptance receipt: "when accepting a case, a public security organ shall prepare a case-acceptance registration form and a case-acceptance receipt, and hand the receipt to the person delivering a suspect, reporting, accusing or exposing". ### 40. Give no money or cards to case-handlers or enforcers: bribing is a crime of yours too, and bribing supervision, enforcement or judicial personnel gets punished heavier
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. It's just not doing it.
- TL;DR: "Spend a little to make the matter go away" is a standalone crime in the Criminal Law - the one caught isn't only the person taking the money. Offering a bribe: up to 3 years. Serious circumstances: 3 to 10; especially serious, 10 years to life. Bribing supervision, administrative-enforcement or judicial personnel is also an expressly heavier tier. Being extorted into giving money without obtaining an improper benefit is not bribery.
- Benefit: Criminal Law Article 389 defines bribery: "giving property to a state functionary in order to seek an improper benefit is the crime of offering a bribe". The same article: "in economic dealings, violating state rules by giving a state functionary property of a relatively large amount, or giving kickbacks or handling fees under various names, is punished as bribery". It also leaves an exception: "giving property to a state functionary due to extortion, without obtaining an improper benefit, is not bribery". Sentencing is written in Article 390 (amended by Criminal Law Amendment XII, effective March 1, 2024): "whoever commits the crime of offering a bribe shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined; whoever seeks an improper benefit through bribery, where the circumstances are serious, or causes heavy losses to the interests of the state, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined; where the circumstances are especially serious, or the losses to the interests of the state are especially heavy, the sentence shall be fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and a fine or confiscation of property shall also be imposed". Seven heavier-punishment circumstances: "(1) offering bribes multiple times or to multiple persons; (2) a state functionary offering a bribe; (3) offering a bribe in a key state project or major project; (4) offering a bribe to seek a position or promotion or transfer in rank; (5) offering a bribe to supervision, administrative-enforcement or judicial personnel; (6) offering a bribe in the fields of ecology and environment, finance, work safety, food and drugs, disaster prevention and relief, social security, education or healthcare, in committing unlawful or criminal activity; (7) using unlawful gains to offer a bribe". The last paragraph leaves a door for voluntary confession: "a briber who voluntarily confesses the bribery before being prosecuted may be given a lighter or mitigated punishment. Among them, where the crime is relatively minor, the confession plays a key role in breaking the investigation or solving a major case, or there is major meritorious service, the punishment may be mitigated or waived" (nationwide)

- Notes: The beneficiary is yourself. What this entry faces is the thought "a little bribe can exempt the punishment". The other side has colleagues, ledgers, cameras and supervision - what you hand over is evidence when they're investigated and criminal evidence of yours, and this kind of bribery is an expressly heavier tier. If the other side genuinely demands favors, the right move is the channel of entry 39 of this chapter (demand the acceptance receipt when reporting): keep the receipt and documents, and report to the supervision organ or the discipline-inspection and supervision commission. The exemption condition in Article 389(3) is "extorted and no improper benefit obtained" - the moment you use the chance to get something you couldn't otherwise get, that condition fails.
