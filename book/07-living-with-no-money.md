# 7. Living with No Money

This chapter counts money and support. How much you can claim, how much can be waived, what services you can get, and what conditions you must meet are all written inside - nothing is converted into lifespan. Policies change every year and differ by place. All amounts and thresholds go by what your locality publishes for the current year. Entry order: no cost, little paperwork, fast results first; entries that build long-term eligibility or vary greatly by locality later; ones based only on experience, without official documents, last. This chapter is China-specific: the hotlines, agencies and statutes named are China's.

### 1. If you lose your job, claim unemployment insurance online first
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. You can file from your phone through any of three entrances: the "Pocket 12333" app, the electronic social-security-card mini-program inside WeChat or Alipay, or the National Social Insurance Public Service Platform. A social security card or ID card is enough, and most regions don't require an in-person visit. The prerequisite is completing unemployment registration first.
- TL;DR: If you and your employer paid in for 1 full year and the separation wasn't your own resignation, you can claim money once unemployment registration is done. 1 to under 5 years of contributions: up to 12 months. 5 to under 10 years: up to 18 months. 10 years or more: up to 24 months. While you draw it, unemployment insurance pays your employee medical insurance for you.
- Benefit: Three conditions must all hold. First, your employer and you together paid unemployment insurance for a cumulative 1 year. Second, the separation wasn't your own initiative (legally, "employment interrupted not of your own will"). Third, you've completed unemployment registration and are willing to look for work. How long you can draw depends on contribution years: 1 to under 5 years, up to 12 months; 5 to under 10 years, up to 18 months; 10 years or more, up to 24 months. The monthly amount is set by provincial governments - provinces differ. The amount sits below the local minimum wage and above urban dibao (subsistence allowance). The national goal is to raise it gradually to 90% of the minimum-wage standard. While drawing unemployment insurance you remain in employee basic medical insurance with full benefits; the premium comes out of the unemployment insurance fund, not your own pocket. In other words, while drawing the money your care still runs through employee medical insurance and you pay no premium yourself (nationwide, regulation in force since 1999)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (1999). Unemployment Insurance Regulation (State Council Order No. 258, Articles 14, 17, 18). <https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=517> ; Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security. - Notes: Voluntary resignation doesn't count as "not of your own will" - no benefit. Employee medical insurance during the claiming period is paid by unemployment insurance for you. Amounts differ a lot by province - go by the place where you're insured. The "90% of minimum wage" figure comes from a 2017 HRSS ministry guidance document. ### 2. For unpaid wages, complain to labor inspection first, then apply for labor arbitration - neither charges a fee, and most cases conclude within months
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free - complaints and arbitration both charge nothing. You need your labor contract, pay slips, attendance records, transfer records and chat records, plus 1 to 3 trips to the HRSS bureau or arbitration commission. Arbitration has a 1-year time limit (legally, the arbitration limitation period). If you're still employed there and wages are being withheld, that 1-year limit doesn't apply. If you've already left, you must file within 1 year of the leaving date. The hard part is trip after trip and document after document - it's easy to lose heart midway.
- TL;DR: Neither route charges money. First call 12333 or complain to labor inspection at the HRSS bureau: after a case is filed, inspection finishes within 60 working days and can order the company to pay by a deadline - overdue payment carries an extra 50% to 100% in compensation. If that fails, apply for labor arbitration, which closes within 45 days. But "case closed" isn't "money arrived": if the company has no assets or the boss has fled, you can win and still get nothing.
- Benefit: Step one is the complaint. Dial 12333, or complain at the local HRSS bureau's labor inspection. The law requires inspection to finish within 60 working days of filing; complicated cases get 30 more working days. Once verified, inspection can order the company to pay by a deadline. If the deadline passes unpaid, an extra compensation of 50% to 100% of the owed amount is added. In 2024, inspection agencies nationwide handled 137 thousand cases and recovered 8.65 billion yuan in wages for 654 thousand people. If you hold an IOU or a wage sheet and the arrears are clear, you can also apply directly to the basic-level court for a payment order - a court-issued payment command, issued within 15 days of acceptance. Step two is arbitration. The law requires closure within 45 days of acceptance, extendable by 15. Wage claimants can apply for advance execution - partial execution before the case fully concludes. Arrears not exceeding 12 times the local monthly minimum wage are final at arbitration: the award is binding and the company can't sue over it. In 2024, 4.257 million cases were handled nationwide and 4.156 million closed. Closed-case amount: 93.47 billion yuan. Mediation success rate 79.6%, arbitration closure rate 98.2%, arbitration finality rate 73.7%. When people say this drags "six months to a year", they mean the portion that goes on to court after arbitration: first instance 6 months (3 under simplified procedure), plus 3 months for second instance. In 2024 courts concluded 88 thousand wage-recovery cases and recovered 28.54 billion yuan; 998 people were sentenced for the crime of refusing to pay labor remuneration (nationwide)

- Notes: This route works, but keep "case closed" and "money arrived" separate. The 98.2% closure rate only means the procedure finished. If the company has no assets or the boss has fled, you can win and still get nothing. Each province's high court and procuratorate set and publish the province's specific amounts within that range - check your province's figure before complaining and raise it in person. Construction-site migrant workers have separate rules on dedicated wage accounts and the general contractor paying first - going after the general contractor works better than going after the labor subcontractor. Migrant workers applying for legal aid over wage claims are not checked for financial hardship. ### 3. If you can't afford a lawsuit, apply for legal aid - wage claims, support payments, and work-injury cases are already inside its scope
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Legal aid charges the aided person nothing.
- TL;DR: Claiming wages, claiming support payments, claiming social-insurance or subsistence benefits, and losses from acting bravely for a just cause are already inside free legal aid's scope. Call 12348, or go to the local legal aid center. Since 2022 you no longer need a financial-hardship certificate from your home registry - a truthful statement of your situation is enough. Defendants facing a possible death sentence, and defendants who are blind, deaf, mute or minors, get a lawyer appointed regardless of finances.
- Benefit: The State Council regulation names the six kinds of civil matters legal aid covers. Its own words: "claims for state compensation according to law; requests for social insurance benefits or minimum living guarantees; requests for pensions or relief payments; requests for support, alimony or maintenance payments; claims for labor remuneration; civil rights arising from acts of bravery for a just cause". In plain terms: state compensation, social insurance or subsistence benefits, pensions and relief, support and alimony, wage claims, and losses from brave acts. On the criminal side, defendants who may be sentenced to death and defendants who are blind, deaf, mute or minors get a lawyer appointed regardless of financial situation.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2003). Legal Aid Regulation (State Council Order No. 385, Articles 10, 11, 12). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6944.htm> ; Ministry of Justice official (2021). Q&A on implementing the Legal Aid Law. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-12/29/content_5665114.htm>
- Notes: The Legal Aid Law took effect January 1, 2022, with one change: the old "submit a financial-hardship certificate" became a truthful statement of your own financial situation - no more trips back to your home registry for a certificate. Entrances are local legal aid centers and public legal-service centers, or call 12348.

### 4. When you hit rock bottom, go to a relief station - food, shelter, and a ticket home
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Go to the local relief administration station yourself and ask for help, or tell a police officer or the civil affairs department and let them take you. On entry you register your name and belongings truthfully. The hard part is saying out loud to someone that you have no money and nowhere to go.
- TL;DR: Relief stations provide food and shelter, send you to hospital if you fall ill, and help contact your family. If you can't afford to get home, they issue a ticket. Stays usually run under 10 days. It's voluntary in, voluntary out - not custody, and no cash is handed out. The ticket is issued to your household-registration place or usual residence - decide where you're going back to before you go.
- Benefit: Relief stations provide food and shelter meeting hygiene requirements. If you fall acutely ill inside, they send you to hospital. They also help contact relatives and your employer. If you can't afford the fare back to your household-registration place or usual residence, they issue a travel voucher - a train or boat ticket. The relief period generally doesn't exceed 10 days; waiting for family pickup, treatment, or placement can extend it. Police and other administrative officers who encounter people begging on the street in the course of duty should tell them they can ask for help; for people with disabilities, minors, the elderly and those with limited mobility, they should guide and escort them there (nationwide, since 2003)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2003). Measures for the Relief and Administration of Urban Vagrants and Beggars with No Means of Support (State Council Order No. 381, Articles 5, 6, 7). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62246.htm> ; Ministry of Civil Affairs (2003). Implementation rules for those Measures (MCA Order No. 24, Articles 11, 12). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62510.htm>
- Notes: Voluntary in, voluntary out - not custody. What you get is basic food and shelter, not cash. The travel voucher is issued to your household-registration place or usual residence - decide where you're going before you ask.

### 5. For work, use the free public employment services and gig-work markets - not fee-charging agencies
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Register at a public employment service agency at county level or above - employment service centers, human-resources markets, that kind of place. Local gig-work markets also take registrations. If you don't know the address, call 12333 and ask.
- TL;DR: Public institutions like employment service centers and human-resources markets charge nothing for job referrals, career guidance, policy consultation, and employment/unemployment registration - and the law expressly forbids them to profit from these services. Gig-market registration and listings are free too. While you're there, complete unemployment registration - claiming unemployment benefits and being recognized as a person with employment difficulties both need it.
- Benefit: The law makes all of these free: employment-policy consultation; information on who is hiring and who is short-staffed; career guidance and job referral; employment and unemployment registration; and employment assistance for people with employment difficulties. These agencies may not engage in for-profit activity - they may not make money from these services. Gig-work listings are registered and published free to the public, already folded into public employment information services. For gig workers who wait long between jobs and those from low-income families, extra help is mandated (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2007, amended 2015). Employment Promotion Law (Article 35). <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647636.htm> ; Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and four other departments (2022). Opinions on strengthening gig-work market development and improving job-seeking and recruitment services (HRSS [2022] No. 38). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-07/09/content_5700177.htm>
- Notes: Unemployment registration is the prerequisite for claiming unemployment insurance and for employment-difficulty recognition - get it done while you're there. If you do gig work paid by the day, check the pay on the spot and keep the other party's contact details and a record of the work.

### 6. After a sudden blow, apply for temporary assistance first
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Apply to the township government or subdistrict office, and explain clearly what happened at home - a fire, a traffic accident, a family member's sudden serious illness, or an unavoidable expense that suddenly jumped. After review and public notice, the county-level civil affairs department approves. Prepare materials per the local checklist; usually 1 to 2 trips.
- TL;DR: When something sudden hits the household - a fire, a crash, a family member's sudden serious illness - and basic living is temporarily crushed, you can apply to the township government or subdistrict office for temporary assistance. You don't have to already be a dibao (subsistence) household. It is usually a one-time payment; amounts vary greatly by locality.
- Benefit: Two kinds of households can receive temporary assistance. One: households that because of an accident or a member's sudden serious illness temporarily can't cover the most basic expenses of food, shelter and care. Two: dibao households whose unavoidable expenses suddenly increase beyond what they can bear. Which events qualify and how much is paid are set and published by local governments at county level or above (national system, amounts set locally, since 2014)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: State Council (2014, revised 2019). Interim Measures for Social Assistance (State Council Order No. 649, Articles 47, 48, 49). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm>
- Notes: You don't have to already be a dibao household. It is usually a one-time payment; amounts differ a lot by place - go by what your locality publishes for the current year.

### 7. If your income falls below the local dibao line, apply for dibao (subsistence allowance)
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. A family member who lives with the household submits a written application to the township government or subdistrict office where the household is registered. If handling it yourself is inconvenient, you can entrust the village committee or residents' committee to do it for you. You must accept income and asset verification. Usually 2 to 3 trips, with ID card, household-registration book and income/asset documents. The hard part is laying your household finances open for inspection and waiting through the public notice.
- TL;DR: Total household income divided by the number of members, below the local dibao line, with household assets also within the rules - and you can apply. At the end of 2024, the national average urban dibao was 798.1 yuan per person per month, rural 593.9 yuan. You must apply where your household is registered. Owning a house, a car or savings, or having children able to support you, may sink the application.
- Benefit: Household income divided by members below the local dibao standard, with assets within the rules, qualifies. At the end of 2024 the national average urban standard was 798.1 yuan per person per month, rural 593.9 yuan. Urban dibao covered 6.250 million people, rural 33.615 million. The standard is set this way: take the locality's per-capita consumption expenditure from the previous year, multiply by a set proportion, and adjust every so often. In plain terms: take what the average local person spent last year and apply a discount (national averages - localities differ)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2014, revised 2019). Interim Measures for Social Assistance (State Council Order No. 649, Articles 9, 10, 11). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm> ; National Bureau of Statistics (2025). Social programs improving, public welfare growing - statistical report on social development since the 14th Five-Year Plan. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202509/content_7042627.htm> ; Ministry of Civil Affairs and three other departments (2024). Guidance on better setting and adjusting subsistence-allowance standards (Min Fa [2024] No. 16). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202403/content_7007237.htm>
- Notes: Dibao is the last safety net, and you apply where your household is registered. A house, car or savings above the local standard, or children able to support you, can sink the application. Once approved, other benefits connect: resident medical-insurance contributions get subsidized (resident medical insurance - entry 9 of this chapter), care can go through medical assistance (entry 10), and legal aid applications skip the financial-hardship check (entry 3).

### 8. If you hold a disability certificate, apply for the two disability allowances
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Take your disability certificate to the subdistrict or township and submit one application.
- TL;DR: People with disabilities in dibao households can receive the living allowance. People assessed at grade 1 or 2 disability who need long-term care can receive the nursing allowance. The two can be drawn together. Standards are set by each province and differ a lot - ask the subdistrict for specifics. Bring the second-generation disability certificate and do it in one visit.
- Benefit: The State Council established two allowances. The first is the living allowance for people with disabilities in hardship: the document names "people with disabilities in dibao households; localities with the means may gradually extend it to low-income and other people with disabilities in hardship" - dibao households first, outward as local finances allow. The second is the nursing allowance for severe disability: the document names "people with severe disability assessed at grade 1 or 2 who need long-term care". The two can be drawn at the same time.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2015). Opinions on fully establishing the living allowance for people with disabilities in hardship and the nursing allowance for severe disability (Guo Fa [2015] No. 52). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2015-09/25/content_10181.htm>
- Notes: Standards are set by provincial governments and differ a lot - tens to hundreds of yuan a month depending on the place. Bring the second-generation disability certificate. If going yourself is inconvenient, you can entrust a family member to do it.

### 9. Don't let resident medical insurance lapse - 400 yuan a year, waived or reduced for hardship households
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Your own share is at least 400 yuan per person per year. That is the 2024-2025 national standard; localities may set it higher. It must be paid during the fixed annual enrollment window. People in extreme hardship (tekun) are fully subsidized - the government pays it all. Dibao recipients get a fixed-amount subsidy - the government pays a set part.
- TL;DR: You pay 400 yuan a year and up; the government adds about 700 yuan per head. Tekun recipients are fully subsidized, dibao recipients fixed-amount. Lapsing has a price: if you miss the enrollment window or don't pay continuously, 3 months after paying your care isn't reimbursed - and each additional lapsed year in principle adds another month.
- Benefit: On top of what you pay, the government adds a per-head subsidy: at least 670 yuan in 2024, at least 700 in 2025 (the 700 figure comes from a Hebei provincial document relaying the national standard). The 2026 government work report says it rises another 24 yuan. For dibao recipients and tekun recipients, qualifying medical expenses not covered after insurance reimbursement get reimbursed again through medical assistance at no less than 70%. The price of lapsing is a waiting period. Miss the enrollment window or pay non-continuously, and a fixed 3-month benefit waiting period follows payment, during which care isn't reimbursed. Each additional lapsed year in principle adds a month (from 2025, nationwide)

### 10. For serious illness, go through medical insurance, critical-illness insurance, medical assistance and cross-region filing in order - don't touch online loans
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. The order is fixed - step by step. Step one: before hospitalization in another province, file through the "National Cross-Region Medical Filing" mini-program in the National Healthcare Security Administration's WeChat official account. Step two: settle directly at discharge. Step three: if your own share after reimbursement is still too heavy, apply to the township government or subdistrict office for medical assistance. Step four: if still employed, take sick leave under the medical-treatment period - the stretch when you're off work sick and can't be fired; work injuries go through work-injury insurance. Step five: if you can't make credit-card payments, negotiate installments with the bank yourself. Step six: if it still isn't enough, fundraise on a personal-help platform designated by the Ministry of Civil Affairs. Materials are mainly the diagnosis certificate, itemized bills and settlement statements - 2 to 4 trips. The hard part is running the procedures trip after trip while ill.
- TL;DR: The order is fixed. Before hospitalization in another province, file in the NHSA's official account; settle directly at discharge without fronting the whole bill. If your share after reimbursement is still unaffordable, apply to the township or subdistrict for medical assistance. Employed people get a 3-to-24-month medical-treatment period by seniority, during which the company can't fire you. Online loans and high-interest private borrowing - don't touch.
- Benefit: The state calls basic medical insurance, critical-illness insurance and medical assistance together the "triple system", catching you layer by layer. Dibao and tekun recipients get qualifying medical expenses assisted at no less than 70%. Seriously ill patients who weren't poor but were made poor by illness can apply for assistance themselves. With cross-region filing done before hospitalization, you settle directly at discharge instead of fronting the full bill and claiming back home. Sick leave comes with a 3-to-24-month medical-treatment period set by work history, during which the company may not terminate your contract, and sick-pay may not fall below 80% of the local minimum wage. Work-injury employees get a suspension-with-pay period - the stretch off work recovering - with original wages and benefits paid monthly by the employer, generally up to 12 months. Credit-card debt beyond your ability to repay, where you are willing to repay, can be negotiated with the bank into personalized installments - a restructured repayment plan of up to 5 years, and once signed the bank must stop collection calls. Online personal-help fundraising platforms must be designated by the Ministry of Civil Affairs; platforms must verify that fundraising information is genuine, and donations may go only to the fundraiser or an account they provide (nationwide)
- Evidence: A

- Notes: What share medical assistance reimburses and its annual cap are set by the place where you're insured. ### 11. If you lose your ID card, replace it immediately - get a temporary one for urgent use
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no benefit=small lens=money -->
- Cost: A document fee applies. It's a statutory charge, the amount set nationally - go by the local public notice. Apply at the police station where your household is registered; one trip.
