# 15. Renting and buying a home

Lens: money. Where renting most easily loses money is the deposit and the agent. Where buying costs most is loan interest - and the things the seller knows that you don't.

### 1. The deposit's amount, return time and deduction cases must be written into the contract
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Ten extra minutes when signing. The hard part is raising it in front of the landlord
- TL;DR: How much the deposit is, when it's returned, and under what conditions it can be docked - all written into the contract before signing. A deduction reason not in the contract can't be used against your deposit at move-out.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation is plain: "where the lessor collects a deposit, the housing-rental contract shall stipulate the deposit's amount, return time, deduction cases and the like. Beyond the cases stipulated in the contract, the lessor may not dock the deposit without justification". In plain words, three things go into the contract: how much is collected, when it's returned, what it can be docked for. A reason not in the contract can't dock your money
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2025). Housing Rental Regulation (State Order No. 812, effective September 15, 2025) (Article 10). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: On move-out day, photograph and film together with the landlord. Get the water, electricity and gas meter readings, plus walls and floors. A deposit docked without cause: complain to the housing-rental authority first. Small amounts go through small-claims litigation - see chapter 8.

### 2. Water or power cut, locks changed, threatening visits to drive you out: call the police and keep evidence first - the regulation bans these ways of forcing you out
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. One police call, done on the spot
- TL;DR: A landlord may not force you to break the lease or move out by cutting water and power, changing locks or threatening visits - the regulation bans it outright. Facing it, call the police first. The response record is your evidence for claiming losses later. Don't fight back physically yourself.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation is plain: "the lessor may not use violence, threats or other illegal means to force the tenant to rescind the housing-rental contract or vacate the rented housing". Vacate means move out. Facing this, call the police first and keep the evidence. Don't fight back physically
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2025). Housing Rental Regulation (State Order No. 812, effective September 15, 2025) (Article 12). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: The police call is for the response record - your evidence when claiming losses later. Notify the housing-rental authority at the same time.

### 3. Agents may not collect rent and deposits on anyone's behalf - pay the landlord directly
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. Verify the payee before paying - minutes. The hard part is withstanding the agent's pitch to route money through them
- TL;DR: By rule, rent and deposit may not be collected or paid through the agent - pay the landlord directly. Money passing through the agent's hands is one more chance for them to run off with it. If they do, your rent is gone and the landlord can still evict you. Before paying, check the payee against the name on the title certificate.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation lists "collecting or paying housing rent and deposits on others' behalf" among prohibited acts for real-estate brokerage agencies. The agent may not collect your rent and deposit for the landlord. Money passing through the agent's hands is one more chance for a run-off
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2025). Housing Rental Regulation (State Order No. 812, effective September 15, 2025) (Article 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Before paying, check the payee against the title certificate. Not that person: ask why, and keep a written authorization. The agent may collect only the intermediary service fee for making the match.

### 4. Renting from a long-lease apartment company: check its supervised funds account first - don't pay a year up front for the discount
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. One check, ten-plus minutes
- TL;DR: Paying a year up front buys only a small discount. If the company collapses, your deposit and the remaining ten-plus months of rent go together. A rent loan bundled in is worse: the apartment company runs, and you keep repaying the loan monthly. Before signing, check whether it has published a supervised funds account.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation requires subleasing housing-rental enterprises to "set up a supervised housing-rental funds account and publish it to the public". Subleasing means the company leases whole units from landlords and sublets to you. The small discount for paying a year up front doesn't cover the risk of losing deposit and remaining rent together when the company runs. A rent loan signed alongside makes the risk bigger
- Evidence: A
- Sources: State Council (2025). Housing Rental Regulation (State Order No. 812, effective September 15, 2025) (Article 19). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: At signing, see whether a loan is bundled in. If an installment app paid the company a full year up front while you repay monthly, the company running away leaves you repaying. Don't sign that contract.

### 5. A home sold mid-lease: the lease stays valid - you don't move
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free
- TL;DR: A home sold during your lease leaves the lease valid. The new owner honors the original contract to its end. No moving, no renegotiating price. The precondition: you already legally live there - keep the contract, transfer records and move-in-date evidence.
- Benefit: The Civil Code has a rule called "sale does not break lease" - selling the home doesn't affect a signed lease. The statute's words: a change of ownership of the leased property during the lease term does not affect the lease contract's validity. A change of ownership means the home changed hands mid-lease. The new owner honors the original contract to its end
- Evidence: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 725). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: The precondition is that you already legally live there - the statute's phrase is lawful possession and use. Keep the contract, transfer records and move-in-date evidence.

### 6. Before signing, verify the title certificate and mortgage status; every payment goes by transfer with a purpose note
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. One check, half an hour
- TL;DR: The two commonest total losses: rent paid to someone who isn't the owner, and a home long mortgaged to a bank and later seized. Before signing, check title and mortgage status at the real-estate registry. Every payment goes by transfer, noted "rent for [home], [month]" - direct evidence in a dispute.
- Benefit: The two commonest total losses come like this. First, rent paid to someone who isn't the owner. Second, a home long mortgaged to a bank, later seized. Noting "rent for [home], [month]" on the transfer makes it direct evidence in a dispute
- Evidence: C
- Sources: Author's experience; no direct literature. Evidence-keeping and transfer notes work the same in the betrothal-gift and IOU entries of chapter 8
- Notes: Title information can be checked at the local real-estate registry. Have the landlord attend the signing in person; if they can't, get a power of attorney. A sublet from a secondary landlord needs the original landlord's written consent.

### 7. With an agent collecting the purchase price on a resale home: it must go through the agent's dedicated transaction-funds account at a bank
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some benefit=big lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is insisting on the dedicated account - possibly arguing a few extra rounds with the agent
- TL;DR: The purchase price is hundreds of thousands to millions - the largest single sum in this book. Where the agent collects it, the rules require it to go through the agent's dedicated bank account - never a WeChat transfer to an individual broker. Also: two agencies cooperating on one deal may charge only one commission; loan-handling and transfer-handling services take separate contracts with fees stated up front.
- Benefit: The regulation is plain: where the parties agree that the brokerage collects and pays transaction funds, the money moves through the client transaction-settlement-funds dedicated deposit account the brokerage opened at a bank. The price goes into a supervised dedicated account - not the agency's own account, and not a broker's personal account
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, NDRC, Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (2011). Measures for real-estate brokerage administration (Order No. 8, amended by Order No. 29 of 2016), Article 24: "where the parties to a real-estate transaction agree that the brokerage collects and pays transaction funds, the funds shall move through the client transaction-settlement-funds dedicated deposit account the brokerage opened at a bank"; Article 18 "real-estate brokerage services use posted pricing... services, content and fee standards displayed prominently at the business premises"; Article 19 "two or more brokerages cooperating on the same transaction may charge commission for only one deal - no extra charge to the client"; Article 17, loan-handling, registration-handling and other additional services "shall be explained to the client with their content and fee standards, and contracted separately with the client's consent". <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm>
- Notes: The purchase price is hundreds of thousands to millions - this book's largest single sum; no WeChat transfers to a broker. Two common overcharges: two agencies jointly doing one deal, which may charge only one commission; and loan-handling or transfer-handling, which are separate services needing separate contracts with fees disclosed up front. Every payment goes by transfer with a purpose note - entry 6 (verify title and mortgage before signing).

### 8. Don't rent a partitioned flat: the smallest rentable unit is the originally designed room - kitchens, bathrooms and balconies may not house anyone
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no benefit=mid lens=money -->
- Cost: Free. The price: on the same budget, living farther out or in a smaller place
- TL;DR: By rule, the smallest rentable unit is the originally designed room. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies and basement storage may not house anyone. When such a flat is investigated, you may have to move. Also: mid-lease, the landlord can't raise the rent unilaterally at will.
- Benefit: The regulation is plain: the smallest rentable unit is the originally designed room - one room may not be partitioned into several for separate rent. Per-capita rented floor area may not fall below the local minimum standard. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies and basement storage may not be rented for people to live in. Mid-lease, the lessor may not unilaterally raise the rent at will
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (2010). Measures for commercial housing rental administration (Order No. 6), Article 8: "rented housing takes the originally designed room as the smallest rentable unit, with per-capita rented floor area no lower than the local people's government's minimum standard. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies and basement storage may not be rented for people to live in"; Article 9: "the lessor shall perform repair duties per the contract and keep the home and its indoor facilities safe... during the lease term, the lessor may not unilaterally raise the rent level at will". <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm>
- Notes: When an illegal partition is investigated, the tenant is the one who moves - and the deposit and paid rent often don't come back. It's also less safe: partition walls often block escape routes, and many people in one flat share one circuit. Three things to check when viewing: whether a wall was added later, whether the room has a window, and whether the meter can carry that many people.
