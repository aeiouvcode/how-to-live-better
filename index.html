How to Live Better

Every entry answers two questions: what does it cost, and what does it buy back.

Who the benefit lands on is also tiered: you first, then your spouse and immediate family, then friends and coworkers, then strangers - lowest is not zero, but the expected payback is small and the risks come with it.
